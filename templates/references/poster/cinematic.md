# Cinematic Poster

## 适用场景

电影质感海报：电影海报、品牌大片视觉、高端品牌广告、史诗级产品发布等需要强烈故事感和戏剧张力的设计场景。

## Prompt 结构

```
[电影场景/主体] + [戏剧光效/氛围] + [故事感构图] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A breathtaking cinematic poster featuring a lone silhouette standing at the edge of a vast cliff overlooking an ocean of clouds at golden hour. Dramatic volumetric light rays pierce through the cloud layer creating a heavenly spotlight effect. Deep warm amber tones blending into cool blue shadows for classic cinematic color grading. The composition uses the rule of thirds with the figure placed in the lower right, leaving the dramatic sky and expansive negative space above for title typography. Epic scale, emotional weight, and visual poetry. cinematic poster design, dramatic lighting, movie poster aesthetic, epic composition, golden hour photography, anamorphic lens flare, film grain texture, 2:3 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Moody cinematic poster with a dark alley scene illuminated by a single shaft of cold moonlight from above. Wet pavement reflecting neon signs in puddles of crimson and teal. Rain droplets frozen mid-air catching the light. The composition draws the eye upward along converging lines of fire escapes and building edges. Vast dark space in the upper half reserved for film title in bold typography. Noir atmosphere with deep shadows, high contrast, and a sense of mystery and tension. cinematic poster design, dramatic lighting, movie poster aesthetic, neo-noir atmosphere, high contrast chiaroscuro, rain-soaked urban scene, 2.39:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Grand cinematic brand poster featuring a product (abstract sleek form) emerging from swirling mist in a vast empty desert at twilight. The horizon line sits low in the frame emphasizing the enormous dramatic sky painted in deep purple, burnt orange, and deep navy. Subtle lens flare and atmospheric haze adding depth and scale. Clean negative space in the upper two-thirds for brand name and tagline. Monumental, awe-inspiring, and deeply atmospheric — the kind of visual that defines a brand. cinematic poster design, dramatic lighting, movie poster aesthetic, epic scale, twilight atmosphere, volumetric fog, premium brand photography, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
cinematic poster design, dramatic lighting, movie poster aesthetic
```

可选增强：`epic composition, golden hour photography, anamorphic lens flare, film grain texture, neo-noir atmosphere, high contrast chiaroscuro, volumetric fog, premium brand photography`

## 注意事项

- **暗调氛围**：电影质感偏爱暗调（dark tones, deep shadows），用少量高亮区域（光柱、反射、霓虹）制造对比
- **戏剧光效**：强调体积光（volumetric light）、伦勃朗光（Rembrandt lighting）、明暗对照法（chiaroscuro）、镜头光晕（lens flare）
- **故事感**：构图要有叙事性——孤独的身影、延伸的道路、神秘的场景，引发观者的好奇心和情感共鸣
- **胶片质感**：加入 "film grain texture"、"anamorphic lens"、"color grading" 等关键词增强电影感
- **宽画幅**：电影感常用 2.39:1（超宽银幕）或 2:3（竖版海报）
