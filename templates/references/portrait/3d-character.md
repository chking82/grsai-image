# 3d-character — 3D角色

## 适用场景
3D 渲染角色、Pixar 风格、游戏角色。

## Prompt 结构
```
A 3D rendered character portrait of [subject description], [physical features], [attire], [pose and framing], [lighting style], [background], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 品牌 3D 代言人
**Prompt：**
```
A 3D character render portrait of an adorable 28-year-old East Asian male brand ambassador character, stylized proportionally with slightly oversized expressive head and warm friendly features, short styled black hair with individual 3D strand rendering and soft specular highlights, large warm brown eyes with realistic iris texture and multiple catchlights, genuine welcoming smile, wearing a fitted navy blue polo shirt with subtle fabric texture and a small brand badge on chest, three-quarter pose from waist up, soft cinematic three-point lighting with warm key light, cool fill light, and subtle rim light for separation, soft gradient blue background with subtle bokeh circles, 3D character render, Pixar-style animation character, soft subsurface scattering, rounded shapes, octane render, smooth PBR materials, studio-quality 3D rendering --ar 1:1
```

## 质量后缀
```
3D character render, Pixar-style animation character, soft subsurface scattering, rounded shapes, octane render, smooth PBR materials
```

## 注意事项
- 3D 材质感：PBR 材质、表面散射（subsurface scattering）
- 柔和光线：三点布光或电影级灯光
- 圆润造型：避免尖锐边缘，保持友好可爱
- 角色个性要通过表情和姿态传达
- 可加入体积光、焦散等特效增加质感
