# Xiaohongshu (RED) Social Media Post

## 适用场景

小红书配图：种草分享、产品推荐、生活方式展示、探店打卡、美妆穿搭等小红书平台内容配图，标准为 3:4 竖版比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[生活场景/主体] + [种草风格/美学] + [3:4 竖版留白] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A beautifully styled flat-lay photograph of a curated skincare collection arranged on a warm marble surface. Soft natural window light streaming from the side creating gentle shadows and highlights. The products are artfully scattered among dried flowers, a small ceramic vase, and a cup of coffee creating a cozy lifestyle atmosphere. Clean open space at the top of the frame reserved for overlay title text like a catchy headline or review score. The overall aesthetic is aspirational yet relatable — the kind of image that makes viewers want to save and try everything shown. xiaohongshu social media post, lifestyle sharing aesthetic, trendy social commerce visual, flat-lay photography, natural window lighting, aspirational lifestyle composition, warm marble surface, 3:4 aspect ratio --v 2

## 注意事项

- **3:4 比例**：小红书推荐图片比例为 3:4（竖版），这是平台信息流中展示效果最佳的尺寸
- **顶部文字空间**：小红书用户习惯在图片上方添加标题文字（如 "必看！"、"测评"、"好物推荐"），prompt 中必须包含 "clean space at the top" 或 "text overlay zone at upper edge"
- **生活感**：小红书的核心调性是 "真实但有品味的生活分享"，强调 "aspirational yet relatable"、"effortless"、"cozy"，而非过度精致的商业感
- **种草逻辑**：图片要让人产生 "我也想要这个" 的欲望，主体要清晰突出，氛围要令人向往
