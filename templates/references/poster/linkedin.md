# LinkedIn Professional Post

## 适用场景

LinkedIn 帖子：行业分享、B2B 内容营销、职业洞察、企业品牌帖子、白皮书/报告推广等专业社交平台配图，标准为 1:1 正方形比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[专业主题/场景] + [1:1 正方形构图] + [商务专业感] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A sophisticated professional post image featuring a modern boardroom table with a laptop displaying an upward-trending analytics dashboard. Soft natural light from large windows creating a bright, optimistic atmosphere. The composition is clean and minimal — just the laptop, the table surface in warm walnut wood, and a single notebook and pen. The upper half of the image is a clean bright area perfect for overlay text such as a key statistic or insight headline. The overall aesthetic is professional without being sterile — warm, modern, and credibility-building. LinkedIn professional post, B2B social media content, corporate social visual, modern workspace photography, analytics dashboard focus, warm walnut surface, clean text overlay zone, professional yet warm aesthetic, data-driven content visual, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

A clean infographic-style square composition featuring three floating cards or panels arranged diagonally, each containing a simple icon (a lightbulb, a handshake, and a rocket). The background is a subtle gradient from soft navy blue to lighter blue. The cards cast soft shadows creating depth and a premium layered effect. The top area above the cards is clean and uncluttered for post title or headline text. The design is simple, scannable, and communicates a structured professional idea — perfect for thought leadership or industry insight posts. LinkedIn professional post, B2B social media content, corporate social visual, infographic-style composition, floating card design, navy gradient background, structured professional layout, clean headline zone, thought leadership aesthetic, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

A premium report cover-style square image featuring a sleek mockup of a business report or whitepaper on a dark charcoal background. The report mockup shows a clean cover page with a minimalist geometric logo and title area. Subtle ambient lighting from above creates a spotlight effect on the report while the edges fade into shadow. The composition is centered and symmetrical communicating authority and professionalism. Clean dark areas surrounding the report mockup provide space for supplementary text like "Download Now" or key finding highlights. LinkedIn professional post, B2B social media content, corporate social visual, report mockup photography, dark charcoal background, spotlight composition, centered symmetrical layout, authority and professionalism, lead generation visual, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
LinkedIn professional post, B2B social media content, corporate social visual
```

可选增强：`modern workspace photography, data-driven content visual, infographic-style composition, thought leadership aesthetic, report mockup photography, authority and professionalism, lead generation visual`

## 注意事项

- **1:1 比例**：LinkedIn 信息流帖子图片以 1:1 正方形展示效果最佳，横版长图在移动端会被裁剪
- **专业感**：LinkedIn 是职业社交平台，图片需要传达专业性、可信度和行业洞察。避免过于花哨或娱乐化的元素，使用 "professional"、"credibility"、"authority" 等描述
- **商务风格**：强调商务场景（boardroom、workspace、analytics、report）、商务配色（navy、charcoal、warm wood tones）、商务构图（clean、minimal、structured）
- **行动号召适配**：LinkedIn 帖子经常包含 "Download Now"、"Read More" 等行动号召，需预留空间
