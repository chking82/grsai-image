# 包装展示

## 适用场景
产品套装展示、品牌 VI、礼盒包装效果。

## Prompt 结构
```
A product packaging display photograph of {packaging description} {open/closed/arranged state}. The packaging features {brand elements — logo, colors, typography, finishing details}. {Products/components visible inside or alongside the packaging}. {Lighting and background description — luxurious, clean, or thematic}. {Camera angle and composition — multi-angle, flat lay, or staged scene}. The overall presentation conveys {brand positioning — premium, eco-friendly, festive, etc.}. Product packaging display, luxury packaging photography, gift box presentation, brand visual identity.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 化妆品礼盒套装，包含精华、面霜、面膜，黑色哑光礼盒烫金Logo
**Prompt：**
```
A product packaging display photograph of a luxury skincare gift set arranged in an elegant open gift box composition. The outer packaging is a substantial rectangular gift box in deep matte black with a soft-touch finish, featuring the brand's logo embossed in shimmering gold foil on the lid. The box is shown open with the lid resting at an angle behind it, revealing the interior — a custom-fitted black foam insert with precisely cut compartments holding each product. Inside, from left to right: a 30ml amber glass dropper bottle of vitamin C serum with a gold collar, a 50ml matte white cream jar with a gold screw-top lid, and three individually sealed sheet mask sachets in matching black-and-gold packaging. A small gold-foil thank-you card rests on top of the masks, reading "Your Skincare Ritual" in elegant typography. The entire arrangement sits on a dark marble surface with a subtle charcoal-and-cream veining pattern. The background is a seamless warm grey gradient that transitions from light at the top to dark at the bottom. Soft, diffused overhead lighting creates gentle highlights on the gold foil elements and the glass bottle, while the matte surfaces absorb light beautifully, creating a rich contrast. Shot from a slightly elevated 45-degree angle with a 50mm lens, deep enough depth of field to keep all products and the packaging clearly in focus. The overall presentation conveys luxury, sophistication, and thoughtful gifting — perfect for a premium beauty brand. Product packaging display, luxury packaging photography, gift box presentation, brand visual identity.
```

The sleeve is shown partially slid open, revealing three individual coffee pouches neatly arranged side by side, each in a matching kraft paper stand-up pouch with a resealable zipper closure. Each pouch has a color-coded label band at the top indicating the origin: a deep red band for "Ethiopia Yirgacheffe · Floral & Citrus," a forest green band for "Colombia Huila · Chocolate & Nut," and a warm amber band for "Sumatra Mandheling · Earthy & Bold." The beans are visible through a small die-cut window shaped like a coffee bean on each pouch. The arrangement rests on a light oak wood table with visible grain, surrounded by natural styling elements — a small ceramic cup of freshly brewed espresso with a layer of crema, a sprig of rosemary, and a few scattered whole coffee beans. The background is a warm, cream-colored linen backdrop. Natural window lighting from the side creates soft shadows and warm tones, emphasizing the organic, sustainable aesthetic. Shot from a slightly overhead flat-lay angle with a 50mm lens, moderate depth of field. The overall presentation conveys sustainability, artisan quality, and the joy of exploring different coffee origins. Product packaging display, luxury packaging photography, gift box presentation, brand visual identity.
```

The entire scene is set on a clean white surface with a subtle light grey shadow gradient. Overhead softbox lighting creates even, shadow-free illumination with just enough directionality to give depth to the box edges and the product's contours. The overall presentation conveys the satisfying experience of premium unboxing, minimalist design philosophy, and attention to every detail. ## 注意事项
- 多角度展示包装（外盒、内衬、产品排列）
- 开箱效果要层次分明
- 品牌元素（Logo、色彩、字体）要一致
- 礼盒质感要通过材质表现（哑光、烫金、烫银等）
- 可以加入品牌 VI 元素（卡片、丝带、标签等）
- 适合礼盒产品展示、节日套装、品牌 VI 页面
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048（方图适合社媒）或 2048x1152
