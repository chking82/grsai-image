# 3D渲染

## 适用场景
概念产品、工业设计、Octane 渲染品质、虚拟产品展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A photorealistic 3D product render of {product description}, rendered with Octane Render and ray tracing. {Material and surface description} with physically based rendering (PBR). {Lighting setup — HDRI, area lights, rim lights, etc.}. The product is {position/angle description} on {surface/environment}. {Camera and rendering details}. Cinematic composition with {mood/atmosphere}. 3D product render, octane render, photorealistic 3D visualization, ray tracing, physically based rendering.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 未来概念无线耳机，流线型设计，半透明外壳，内部LED发光
**Prompt：**
```
A photorealistic 3D product render of a futuristic concept wireless earbud with an organic, fluid form — a seamless teardrop shape crafted from semi-transparent frosted polycarbonate that reveals glowing internal LED elements casting a soft blue-white light. Rendered with Octane Render and ray tracing. The semi-transparent shell features a gradient from opaque white at the outer shell to increasingly transparent toward the inner acoustic chamber, where a miniaturized 15mm planar magnetic driver is visible. Soft caustic light patterns refract through the translucent material, creating an ethereal glow. The product is floating slightly above a dark reflective obsidian surface, with a subtle volumetric light beam illuminating it from above. Cinematic three-point lighting with a cool blue key light, warm amber rim light, and soft fill from below. Camera angle: dramatic 3/4 view with a 85mm virtual lens, shallow depth of field. Rendered at 8K resolution with full global illumination, subsurface scattering, and physically based rendering. Cinematic composition with a dark, moody atmosphere that emphasizes the futuristic glow and premium materials. 3D product render, octane render, photorealistic 3D visualization, ray tracing, physically based rendering.
```

Camera angle: low angle shot with a 35mm virtual lens, deep depth of field capturing the entire car in sharp focus. Rendered at 8K resolution with full global illumination, anisotropic reflections on the paint, and physically based rendering. Cinematic composition with a cyberpunk-meets-luxury atmosphere. 3D product render, octane render, photorealistic 3D visualization, ray tracing, physically based rendering.

## 质量后缀
```
3D product render, octane render, photorealistic 3D visualization, ray tracing, physically based rendering
```

## 注意事项
- 强调渲染引擎和渲染技术（Octane、光线追踪、PBR）
- 材质描述要精确（折射、反射、次表面散射等光学属性）
- 可以创造现实中不存在的产品或设计
- 灯光可以超现实、电影化
- 适合概念产品发布、工业设计展示、众筹页面
- 可以加入特殊光学效果（全息、发光、折射等）
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）
