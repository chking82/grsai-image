# Combination Mark Logo — 组合 Logo

## 适用场景
图形 + 文字的组合标识，兼具视觉识别力和品牌名称传达。最常见的 Logo 形式，适合绝大多数品牌。

## Prompt 结构
```
A combination mark logo for {brand/industry description}, consisting of {symbol description} paired with {text treatment — brand name in font style}. The symbol {position — above/left of/integrated with} the text, {color relationship — same color scheme/complementary colors}. The overall composition {layout style — balanced horizontal/stacked vertical/diagonal dynamic}. Combination mark logo, integrated text and symbol design, cohesive brand identity --ar 16:9
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 品牌组合 Logo，左侧是抽象树叶图形，右侧是品牌名 "绿叶家居"，深绿色，水平排列
**Prompt：**
```
A combination mark logo for an eco-friendly home goods brand, consisting of a stylized leaf symbol positioned to the left of the brand name text in a balanced horizontal layout. The leaf symbol is a simple elegant shape — a single leaf tilted at approximately 15 degrees to the right, with a subtle center vein line and three pairs of gentle lobes, rendered as a solid fill in deep forest green (#2D5A27). The leaf has clean smooth curves with no sharp corners except the tip, creating an organic approachable feel. To the right of the leaf, the brand name "绿叶家居" is rendered in a clean modern sans-serif Chinese typeface in the same forest green color, with a smaller English subtitle "GREENLEAF HOME" underneath in a lighter weight uppercase sans-serif. The leaf symbol height matches the Chinese character height precisely, creating visual alignment. The spacing between the leaf and text is approximately one character width. The entire composition sits on a pure white background. Warm organic brand aesthetic conveying natural materials and sustainable living, balanced proportions between symbol and text elements, combination mark logo, integrated text and symbol design, cohesive brand identity.
```

Above the crossed utensils, the word "MAISON" follows the upper arc of the circle in gold uppercase serif letters, and below the utensils, "BISTRO" follows the lower arc in the same gold typeface. Two small gold dots separate the upper and lower text on the left and right sides of the circle. The entire emblem sits on a clean white background with no additional decorative elements. Classic European restaurant aesthetic conveying tradition and quality, balanced circular composition with clear hierarchy from outer ring to center symbol to text, combination mark logo, integrated text and symbol design, cohesive brand identity.

### 示例 3

## 注意事项
- **比例协调**：图形和文字的大小比例要协调，通常图形稍大于文字高度
- **排列方式**：水平排列适合长条场景，堆叠排列适合方形场景（如社媒头像）
- **颜色统一**：图形和文字使用相同色系，保持视觉统一感
- **距离适当**：图形和文字之间要有呼吸空间，约等于一个字符宽度
- **独立使用**：好的组合 Logo 中，图形和文字都应该能单独使用
- **中文适配**：中文字体选择比英文更受限，建议使用专业中文字体
- **多版本输出**：设计时应考虑横版、竖版、纯图形、纯文字多个变体
- **注册保护**：组合 Logo 在商标注册中保护范围更广
