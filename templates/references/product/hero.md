# 电商主图

## 适用场景
商品列表页主图、首图展示、核心产品视觉呈现。

## Prompt 结构
```
A hero product photograph of {product description} dominating the visual center of the frame. The product is {position and angle description} with {key selling point visualized}. {Background description — bold color, gradient, or dynamic backdrop}. Strong visual impact with {color scheme / contrast}. The product fills {proportion} of the frame, immediately drawing attention to {specific feature}. Dramatic {lighting style} creates compelling contrast and depth. Hero product shot, e-commerce main image, commercial product photography, attention-grabbing composition.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 新品首发运动鞋，白色鞋面配彩色气垫鞋底，科技感设计
**Prompt：**
```
A hero product photograph of a newly launched high-tech running shoe with a pristine white engineered mesh upper and a striking multi-color visible air cushion sole in gradient tones of electric blue, neon green, and hot pink, dominating the visual center of the frame. The shoe is positioned at a dynamic 45-degree angle with the toe pointing toward the viewer, showcasing the full side profile and the innovative air cushion technology. The translucent air sole is backlit from below, making the colorful gas chambers glow vividly against a deep navy to black gradient background. Strong visual impact with high contrast between the bright white shoe and the dark background. The shoe fills 60% of the frame, immediately drawing attention to the air cushion sole and the sleek aerodynamic silhouette. Dramatic rim lighting from behind creates a glowing outline effect, while a soft key light from above illuminates the mesh texture and the reflective 3M logo on the heel. Hero product shot, e-commerce main image, commercial product photography, attention-grabbing composition.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 爆款手持小风扇，马卡龙色系，迷你便携设计
**Prompt：**
```
A hero product photograph of a bestselling mini handheld fan in a pastel mint green macaron colorway, dominating the visual center of the frame. The fan — a compact cylindrical body with a protective mesh grille and a detachable wrist strap — is shown at a slightly tilted angle with the fan blades visible through the semi-transparent grille, capturing the moment of spinning motion with a slight motion blur on the blades. A soft breeze effect is suggested by gently fluttering ribbon streamers nearby. Set against a vibrant coral-to-yellow radial gradient background that evokes summer warmth and cooling relief. Strong visual impact with the complementary color contrast between mint green and coral. The fan fills 50% of the frame, immediately drawing attention to its compact, portable size and the cute pastel aesthetic. Bright, cheerful studio lighting with a large softbox creating soft, inviting shadows and specular highlights on the glossy plastic surfaces. Small decorative elements — a few floating water droplets and a subtle ice crystal pattern — reinforce the cooling theme without distracting from the product. Hero product shot, e-commerce main image, commercial product photography, attention-grabbing composition.
```

## 质量后缀
```
hero product shot, e-commerce main image, commercial product photography, attention-grabbing composition
```

## 注意事项
- 产品必须占据视觉中心，构图要大胆、有冲击力
- 突出核心卖点，让用户一眼看到产品亮点
- 背景可以用鲜艳色彩或渐变，创造视觉对比
- 产品占比要大（通常占画面50-70%）
- 灯光要戏剧化，创造强烈的明暗对比
- 适合商品列表页、首图展示、促销广告
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048（方图适合商品列表）
