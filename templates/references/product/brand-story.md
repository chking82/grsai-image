# 品牌故事图

## 适用场景
品牌介绍模块、品牌理念视觉化、品牌历史展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A brand storytelling photograph depicting {scene/subject related to brand heritage/craftsmanship}. {Environment and setting description with historical/traditional elements}. {Characters/subjects description — artisan, craftsman, founder, etc.} engaged in {specific action/process}. {Lighting and atmosphere — warm, nostalgic, cinematic, etc.}. {Composition and camera perspective}. The image conveys {emotional theme — heritage, craftsmanship, dedication, tradition, innovation}. Brand storytelling imagery, brand heritage visualization, narrative product photography, editorial quality.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 手工制鞋匠人在传统工坊中缝制皮鞋，温暖灯光，百年传承
**Prompt：**
```
A brand storytelling photograph depicting a master shoemaker at work in a traditional leather craft workshop that has been operating for over a century. The environment is filled with heritage elements — wooden shelves lined with leather hides in various shades of brown and tan, rows of hand tools (lasting pliers, awls, bone folders, and hammers) hanging on a pegboard wall worn smooth by decades of use, and a vintage Singer sewing machine on a cast-iron table. The craftsman — a man in his 60s with weathered hands, wearing a leather apron — sits at his workbench under the warm glow of an angled desk lamp, carefully hand-stitching the welt of a Goodyear-welted oxford shoe using traditional waxed thread. His focused expression and the precise positioning of his hands convey decades of accumulated skill. The lighting is warm and cinematic — the desk lamp creates a pool of golden light on the work area, while the rest of the workshop falls into soft shadow, with light from a high window catching dust motes in the air. Shot from a medium distance with a 50mm lens at eye level, placing the viewer in the workshop as an observer. Shallow depth of field keeps the craftsman's hands and the shoe in sharp focus while the background tools and shelves create a rich, atmospheric context. The image conveys heritage, artisan dedication, the beauty of traditional craftsmanship, and a century of accumulated knowledge passed down through generations. Brand storytelling imagery, brand heritage visualization, narrative product photography, editorial quality.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 茶叶品牌创始人探访云南古茶山，清晨云雾缭绕，采摘百年古树茶叶
**Prompt：**
```
A brand storytelling photograph depicting a tea brand founder visiting an ancient tea mountain in Yunnan, China, during the ethereal early morning hours. The environment is mystical and primordial — towering ancient tea trees, some over 300 years old with gnarled trunks thick as a person's torso, rising from terraced hillsides covered in thick morning mist. Wisps of cloud drift between the trees, creating layers of depth and a dreamlike atmosphere. The founder — a woman in her 40s wearing simple, natural linen clothing — stands on a narrow stone path between the trees, gently reaching up to pluck the youngest tea leaves (one bud, two leaves) with practiced care. Her expression is one of deep respect and connection to the ancient trees. In the background, the silhouettes of local tea farmers can be seen moving through the mist, carrying traditional bamboo baskets on their backs. The lighting is the soft, diffused golden light of dawn breaking through the mist and canopy — creating god rays filtering through the tea leaves, a warm backlight that outlines the founder's figure, and a cool, misty ambience in the shadows. Shot with a 85mm lens from a slightly elevated angle, with moderate depth of field keeping the founder and the ancient tea tree she's harvesting from in focus while the misty mountain backdrop creates a painterly softness. The image conveys a deep respect for nature, the authenticity of sourcing, the centuries-old tradition of tea culture, and a brand's commitment to the finest origins. Brand storytelling imagery, brand heritage visualization, narrative product photography, editorial quality.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 陶瓷品牌窑炉烧制场景，匠人正在从1300度高温窑中取出成品瓷器
**Prompt：**
```
A brand storytelling photograph depicting a ceramic artisan at the dramatic moment of opening a traditional wood-fired kiln after a 36-hour firing at 1300°C. The environment is an ancient ceramic workshop with soot-blackened brick walls, stacks of raw clay blocks drying on wooden racks, and shelves of unfired greenware waiting for their turn in the kiln. The kiln itself is a massive dome-shaped structure built from refractory bricks, its iron door glowing red-orange from the residual heat, with visible heat waves shimmering in the air. The artisan — a man in his 50s wearing heat-resistant gloves and a canvas apron — carefully uses long iron tongs to pull a glowing-hot porcelain vase from the kiln's interior. The vase, still radiating heat, shows the first glimpses of a stunning celadon glaze that has transformed from dull grey powder to a luminous jade-green during the firing. The intense warmth of the kiln interior casts an orange-red glow across the artisan's face and arms, while the workshop beyond is dimly lit by a single skylight. The contrast between the fiery kiln glow and the cool ambient light creates a powerful visual tension. Shot with a 35mm lens from a medium-close perspective, with the artisan and the emerging vase in sharp focus and the kiln interior creating a dramatic, fiery backdrop. The image conveys the alchemy of ceramic art, the patience of waiting 36 hours for a single moment of revelation, the fusion of fire and earth, and the irreplaceable value of handcrafted objects. Brand storytelling imagery, brand heritage visualization, narrative product photography, editorial quality.
```

## 注意事项
- 强调情感连接和品牌价值观
- 加入历史元素、传统工艺场景
- 匠人/创始人/工匠人物形象增加人情味
- 灯光偏暖、偏电影化，营造氛围
- 环境细节要丰富，传达历史感和传承感
- 产品可以不作为直接主体，而是融入故事
- 适合品牌介绍页、关于我们页面、品牌手册
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9 电影感）
