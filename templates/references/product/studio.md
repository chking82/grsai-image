# 专业摄影棚

## 适用场景
高端产品展示、专业摄影棚品质、商业广告级产品图。

## Prompt 结构
```
A professional studio photography shot of {product description}, captured in a high-end commercial photography studio. Dramatic and precisely controlled lighting with {lighting style}. The product is displayed on {surface/prop} with {background description}. Carefully crafted light reflections reveal the premium {material/texture} of the product. Shallow depth of field with {camera/lens effect}. Sophisticated composition following {composition rule}. Commercial advertising quality, luxury product presentation.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 高端瑞士机械手表，玫瑰金表壳，黑色鳄鱼皮表带，蓝宝石表镜
**Prompt：**
```
A professional studio photography shot of a luxury Swiss mechanical watch with an 18K rose gold case, black alligator leather strap, and sapphire crystal glass, captured in a high-end commercial photography studio. Dramatic and precisely controlled lighting with a three-point softbox setup creating warm golden highlights on the rose gold case and deep, rich shadows that emphasize the watch's premium build. The watch is displayed on a polished dark walnut surface with a deep charcoal gradient backdrop. Carefully crafted light reflections reveal the intricate details of the dial — applied hour markers, skeleton sub-dials, and the visible movement through the exhibition caseback. Shallow depth of field with a 100mm macro lens rendering the crown and lugs in razor-sharp focus while the strap gracefully blurs into the background. Sophisticated composition following the golden ratio, with the watch face positioned at the power point. Commercial advertising quality, luxury product presentation.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 奢侈品牌女包，酒红色牛皮，金色五金
**Prompt：**
```
A professional studio photography shot of a luxury designer handbag crafted from deep burgundy full-grain calfskin leather with polished gold-tone hardware, captured in a high-end commercial photography studio. Dramatic and precisely controlled lighting with a large overhead diffusion panel and two side strip boxes, creating elegant gradient reflections across the smooth leather surface and brilliant specular highlights on the gold buckle and chain strap. The handbag is displayed on a white marble pedestal against a soft warm grey seamless backdrop. Carefully crafted light reflections reveal the supple grain texture of the leather, the precision topstitching, and the mirror-like polish of the metal hardware. Shallow depth of field with a 85mm portrait lens rendering the front flap and clasp in stunning detail while the back panel softly falls away. Sophisticated composition with the bag angled at 45 degrees, filling two-thirds of the frame. Commercial advertising quality, luxury product presentation.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 设计师款太阳镜，钛合金镜架，渐变偏光镜片
**Prompt：**
```
A professional studio photography shot of a pair of designer sunglasses featuring ultra-light titanium alloy frames and gradient polarized lenses transitioning from dark charcoal to warm amber, captured in a high-end commercial photography studio. Dramatic and precisely controlled lighting with a top-down beauty dish and two silver reflectors creating clean, controlled reflections in the lenses that showcase the gradient tint without obscuring the product. The sunglasses are positioned flat on a frosted acrylic display panel suspended above a dark navy background, with the arms elegantly spread open. Carefully crafted light reflections reveal the brushed titanium finish on the frames, the precision hinge mechanism, and the smooth color transition across the lenses. Shallow depth of field with a 90mm macro lens keeping the bridge and lens detail tack-sharp. Minimalist composition with generous negative space, editorial fashion photography aesthetic. Commercial advertising quality, luxury product presentation.
```

## 质量后缀
```
professional studio photography, commercial advertising quality, controlled studio lighting, premium product presentation
```

## 注意事项
- 强调光影层次和材质表现
- 描述具体的布光方式（三点布光、柔光箱、反光板等）
- 使用镜头术语（景深、焦段）增加真实感
- 背景可以是渐变色或深色质感背景，不是纯白
- 注重产品材质的细节描述（金属光泽、皮革纹理等）
- 构图要讲究，可提及黄金分割、三分法等
- 适合高端品牌广告、奢侈品宣传等场景
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048 或 2048x1152（16:9）
