# Food Map — 美食地图

## 适用场景
城市美食指南、旅游攻略中的美食推荐、美食博主内容、餐饮品牌城市布局展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A hand-drawn illustrated food map of {city/region name}, showing {food categories or specific dishes} mapped across {area description}. The map features {map style — illustrated landmarks/simplified streets/neighborhood zones}. {Food illustration style — watercolor/flat vector/cute cartoon}. {Color palette}. Food map illustration, culinary city guide, hand-drawn restaurant map --ar 16:9
```

Each food illustration is rendered in a cute watercolor style with warm appetizing colors — deep reds for spicy dishes, golden yellows for noodle dishes, translucent whites for dumplings. The compass rose in the corner is a cute panda holding a directional sign. Overall warm inviting color palette with reds, oranges, and greens dominating, creating a mouthwatering and fun guide. Food map illustration, culinary city guide, hand-drawn restaurant map.

## 注意事项
- **食物优先**：美食地图的主角是食物，地图是载体——食物插画要精细诱人
- **标注清晰**：每个美食点要有名称（中英文）和简要描述
- **地理准确**：地标和河流位置要大致准确，让读者有方向感
- **蒸汽效果**：热食一定要画蒸汽线——这是让食物看起来好吃的秘诀
- **地方特色**：加入当地标志性元素（熊猫-成都、樱花-东京、榕树-广州）
- **色彩温暖**：暖色调让食物看起来更美味，地图整体色调不要冷
- **留白填充**：用食材小图标（辣椒、香草、食材）填充空白区域增加趣味
- **比例适中**：食物插画不要太大（遮挡地图）也不要太小（看不清）
