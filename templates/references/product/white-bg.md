# 白底产品图

## 适用场景
电商产品主图、纯白底产品拍摄、产品 catalog 图。

## Prompt 结构
```
A professional e-commerce product photograph of {product description}, placed on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). The product is fully isolated with no shadows, no reflections, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Studio-quality lighting with even illumination, sharp focus on the product, high resolution, clean edges. The product should be centered in frame with balanced composition. Commercial catalog quality, ready for online store listing.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 保温杯，304不锈钢，磨砂黑色，500ml容量
**Prompt：**
```
A professional e-commerce product photograph of a 500ml vacuum-insulated thermos bottle made of 304 stainless steel with a matte black finish, placed on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). The product is fully isolated with no shadows, no reflections, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Studio-quality lighting with even illumination, sharp focus on the bottle body and lid details, high resolution, clean edges. The thermos is centered in frame with balanced composition, photographed from a slight 3/4 angle to show both the front profile and the cap. Commercial catalog quality, ready for online store listing.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 手机壳，透明硅胶材质，带有彩色花卉图案
**Prompt：**
```
A professional e-commerce product photograph of a clear transparent silicone phone case decorated with a colorful floral pattern featuring pink peonies, yellow daisies, and green leaves, placed on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). The product is fully isolated with no shadows, no reflections, no text, no logos, no watermarks. The transparent silicone material shows natural translucency with soft edge highlights. Studio-quality lighting with even illumination, sharp focus on the floral print details, high resolution, clean edges. The phone case is displayed flat, angled slightly to reveal both the back design and the raised protective bezel edges. Commercial catalog quality, ready for online store listing.
```

## 质量后缀
```
professional e-commerce product photography, clean isolated product shot, commercial catalog quality, sharp focus on product
```

## 注意事项
- 必须使用 "pure white background"，不能用其他颜色背景
- 强调 "isolated product"，确保产品独立干净
- 明确禁止文字、logo、水印等干扰元素
- 产品必须居中对齐，构图平衡
- 光线要均匀，避免过暗或过曝区域
- 适合批量生成电商 catalog 图片
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048 或 2048x1152（16:9）
