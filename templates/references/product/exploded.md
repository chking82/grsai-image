# 爆炸图/分解图

## 适用场景
产品结构展示、专利图、技术说明图、零部件分解展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A technical exploded view illustration of {product description}, showing all major internal and external components separated and floating in 3D space. Each component is {arrangement description} with clear spatial relationships. {Component details — materials, colors, shapes}. Subtle guide lines or connection indicators show how the parts assemble. {Background description, typically dark or gradient}. Precision engineering illustration style with photorealistic rendering, clean lines, and accurate proportions. Technical visualization quality.
```

```
A technical exploded view illustration of a pair of true wireless earbuds with a compact charging case, showing all major internal and external components separated and floating in 3D space along a vertical axis. From top to bottom: the glossy white charging case lid, the magnetic hinge assembly, the inner charging compartment with gold-plated pogo pin contacts, the lithium polymer battery pack, the main PCB with Bluetooth 5.3 chipset and tiny surface-mount components, the driver unit with a 12mm dynamic cone, the acoustic mesh and silicone ear tips. On either side, the left and right earbud housings are shown with their internal wiring, MEMS microphones, and touch sensor ICs. Each component is spaced evenly with consistent gaps, with subtle dashed guide lines and thin blue connection arrows showing how the parts assemble into the final product. Set against a deep navy to charcoal gradient background with soft volumetric lighting highlighting each component's material — glossy ABS plastic, brushed metal contacts, transparent acoustic mesh, and matte silicone. Precision engineering illustration style with photorealistic rendering, clean lines, and accurate proportions. Technical visualization quality.
```

Precision engineering illustration style with photorealistic rendering, watchmaker-level accuracy, and museum-quality detail. Technical visualization quality.

## 质量后缀
```
technical illustration, exploded view diagram, component breakdown, engineering visualization
```

## 注意事项
- 组件要悬浮排列，有清晰的空间层次感
- 连接关系要用线条或箭头标示
- 材质表现要精准（金属、塑料、玻璃、硅胶等）
- 背景通常为深色以突出组件
- 比例要准确，符合工程图标准
- 适合专利申请书、技术文档、产品详情页结构展示
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）或 2048x2048
