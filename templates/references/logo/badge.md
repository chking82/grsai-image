# Badge / Seal Logo — 徽章/印章 Logo

## 适用场景
圆形或盾形徽章风格的 Logo，带有传统权威感和手工品质感。适合学校、俱乐部、精酿啤酒、咖啡店、手工品牌等。

## Prompt 结构
```
A badge logo design for {brand/industry description}, enclosed within {shape — circular seal/shield/crest/hexagonal frame}. The design features {central symbol}, with {text arrangement — curved along upper edge/lower edge/split top and bottom} reading "{brand name}". {Color scheme}, {decorative elements — stars/lines/ribbons/borders}. Badge emblem logo, seal logo design, traditional brand mark --ar 1:1
```

Along the lower arc, the founding year "1952" is displayed in gold serif numerals. Two small gold five-pointed stars separate the upper and lower text on the left and right sides. The entire emblem on a white background. Traditional academic aesthetic conveying heritage and knowledge, perfectly symmetrical circular composition with clear hierarchy, badge emblem logo, seal logo design, traditional brand mark.

## 注意事项
- **传统感**：徽章 Logo 自带权威和传承感，适合需要建立信任的品牌
- **环形文字**：弧形排列的文字是徽章的标志性特征，必须沿圆弧排列
- **中心图案**：中心图形要简洁有力，不要堆砌太多元素
- **边框层次**：通常有外边框 + 内边框的双层结构，增加精致感
- **分隔元素**：上下文字之间用星号、圆点或短线分隔
- **色彩传统**：深蓝、深红、墨绿、金色、黑色等经典配色最常用
- **应用场景**：特别适合印在圆形物品上（杯垫、瓶盖、印章、杯套）
- **现代简化**：传统徽章容易过于复杂，现代版本应大幅简化细节
