# Wordmark Logo — 文字型 Logo

## 适用场景
以品牌名称本身作为 Logo 的设计，适合名称简洁、有辨识度的品牌。适合科技公司、时尚品牌、食品饮料等。

## Prompt 结构
```
A wordmark logo design for "{brand name}", a {industry/product description}. The typography features {font style — custom geometric sans-serif/elegant serif/hand-drawn lettering}, with {special treatment — unique letter connection/modified character/custom ligature}. {Color scheme}, {additional details — spacing/weight/baseline treatment}. Typography-based brand identity, custom lettering logo, professional brand wordmark --ar 16:9
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 科技公司文字 Logo "NEXUS"，现代无衬线字体，字母 N 和 X 有连接设计，深蓝色
**Prompt：**
```
A wordmark logo design for "NEXUS", a technology infrastructure company. The typography is a custom geometric sans-serif typeface with uniform stroke weight and clean construction, where the diagonal strokes of the N and X share a continuous flowing connection — the right diagonal of the N extends seamlessly into the left diagonal of the X, creating a subtle bridge between the two letters. The letters are rendered in a bold weight (approximately 700) with generous letter-spacing of about 8% of letter width, sitting on a strong baseline with slightly rounded bottom corners for a modern touch. The entire wordmark is in deep navy blue (#1E3A5F) on a pure white background, with no additional symbols or icons. The U has a slightly flattened bottom creating a stable platform effect, and the S features perfectly balanced curves with consistent inner and outer radii. Clean, modern corporate technology aesthetic designed for high legibility at all sizes, typography-based brand identity, custom lettering logo, professional brand wordmark.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 咖啡品牌文字 Logo "BLOOM"，优雅手写体风格，花体字母 B，墨绿色 + 金色点缀
**Prompt：**
```
A wordmark logo design for "BLOOM", an artisan coffee brand. The typography features an elegant hand-drawn script style with flowing cursive letterforms, where the capital B has an elaborate decorative loop at the top that echoes a coffee bean shape, and the lowercase letters flow into each other with natural connecting strokes. The lettering has organic, slightly varied stroke widths mimicking brush-pen calligraphy, with thicker downstrokes and thinner upstrokes. The O has a small golden dot inside its center resembling a coffee droplet. The wordmark is rendered in rich forest green (#2C4A3E) with a subtle golden accent (#C9A96E) on the decorative B loop and the inner O dot, on a warm cream background. The overall baseline has a gentle upward curve of about 3 degrees giving a sense of lift and energy. Artisan craft aesthetic with premium positioning, balanced between handmade warmth and professional polish, typography-based brand identity, custom lettering logo, professional brand wordmark.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 健身品牌文字 Logo "APEX"，粗壮的块状字体，字母 A 的横杠用斜线替代，橙红色
**Prompt：**
```
A wordmark logo design for "APEX", a fitness and athletic performance brand. The typography is an ultra-bold slab-serif typeface with extremely heavy stroke weight and blocky geometric construction, conveying raw power and strength. The capital A has no horizontal crossbar — instead, the two legs are connected by a diagonal slash at a 30-degree angle rising from left to right, creating a dynamic sense of upward movement and peak achievement. The P features an oversized enclosed counter that nearly touches the stem, the E has three equal-length horizontal arms with tight spacing, and the X is constructed from two thick crossing diagonals forming a sharp central intersection point. The entire wordmark in vibrant orange-red (#E85D26) on a dark charcoal background (#1A1A1A), with tight letter-spacing creating a solid block-like appearance. The font has slightly beveled top edges at 5-degree angles adding subtle dimensionality. Aggressive athletic energy with premium sports brand feel, typography-based brand identity, custom lettering logo, professional brand wordmark.
```

## 注意事项
- **字体即品牌**：文字型 Logo 中字体选择就是品牌个性的核心表达
- **独特性**：必须有一个或多个字母的特殊处理，否则与系统字体无异
- **可读性优先**：再怎么艺术化，品牌名称也必须清晰可认
- **间距是关键**：字母间距（kerning）直接影响整体质感
- **避免过于花哨**：手写体适合温暖品牌，科技公司应选几何字体
- **色彩克制**：文字型 Logo 通常 1-2 色即可，过多色彩分散注意力
- **全大写 vs 混合**：全大写更权威，首字母大写更亲和，全小写更现代
- **中文品牌注意**：中文字体设计难度大，建议搭配英文名称使用
