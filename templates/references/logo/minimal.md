# Minimal Logo — 极简 Logo

## 适用场景
需要简洁、现代、易识别的品牌标识设计。适合科技公司、SaaS 产品、互联网品牌等追求现代感的场景。

## Prompt 结构
```
A minimalist logo design for {brand/industry description}, featuring {core visual concept — simple geometric shape/abstract symbol/clean icon}. The design uses {color scheme — monochrome/two-tone/subtle accent}, {line style — thin uniform strokes/solid filled shapes/negative space technique}. Clean geometric logo, modern brand identity, scalable vector aesthetic, white background --ar 1:1
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 科技公司极简 Logo，云朵 + 闪电组合，蓝白配色，扁平化设计
**Prompt：**
```
A minimalist logo design for a cloud computing technology company, featuring a simple geometric cloud outline with a clean lightning bolt symbol integrated inside using negative space technique. The cloud shape is formed by three overlapping circular arcs creating a smooth continuous outline, while the lightning bolt is a sharp diagonal stroke in white cutting through the center, creating a striking contrast. The design uses a vibrant sky blue (#3B82F6) for the cloud outline on a pure white background, with the lightning bolt in solid white. Ultra-clean geometric construction with precise curves and angles, flat design with no gradients or shadows, perfectly balanced proportions with the lightning bolt centered and sized at 60% of the cloud height. Modern tech startup aesthetic, scalable vector design suitable for app icons and favicon, minimalist logo design, modern brand identity, clean geometric logo.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** SaaS 产品图标 Logo，字母 M 抽象化，黑色单色，线条极简
**Prompt：**
```
A minimalist logo design for a SaaS analytics product, featuring an abstract letterform M constructed from three clean vertical strokes of uniform thickness connected by precise diagonal bridges, creating a modern geometric interpretation of the letter. The entire mark is drawn with consistent 12px stroke width, with the outer strokes extending slightly above the inner stroke creating a subtle mountain-like peak silhouette. Pure solid black (#000000) on a white background, no color variations, no gradients, no decorative elements. The design is perfectly symmetrical with mathematical precision, each angle at exactly 45 degrees, all corners crisp and sharp. Ultra-modern corporate software aesthetic, designed to scale cleanly from 16px favicon to billboard size, minimalist logo design, modern brand identity, clean geometric logo.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 环保品牌极简 Logo，一片叶子的轮廓，绿色 + 白色，负空间设计
**Prompt：**
```
A minimalist logo design for an eco-friendly brand, featuring a single leaf silhouette created entirely through negative space — a solid emerald green circle with a leaf-shaped cutout in the center. The leaf form is a simple organic shape with a central vein and three pairs of subtle lobes, tapering to a gentle point at the top with a short stem extending downward. The cutout is pure white against a rich forest green (#2D5A27) circular backdrop, creating an immediate visual impact through the contrast of positive and negative space. The leaf is perfectly centered within the circle, sized at approximately 70% of the circle diameter, with even margins on all sides. Clean organic geometry balanced with modern corporate sensibility, no text elements, no additional decorative shapes, purely the leaf-in-circle concept. Sustainable brand aesthetic suitable for packaging, website, and print materials, minimalist logo design, modern brand identity, clean geometric logo.
```

## 注意事项
- **少即是多**：极简 Logo 的核心是减法，去掉一切不必要的装饰
- **可缩放性**：设计必须在 16px 到 10m 范围内都清晰可辨
- **负空间是利器**：巧妙利用负空间可以创造双重含义
- **单色优先**：好的极简 Logo 应在单色下依然有效
- **几何精确**：线条粗细一致、角度精确、对称平衡
- **避免渐变和阴影**：扁平化设计更符合极简美学
- **测试小尺寸**：务必在手机屏幕尺寸下验证可识别性
- **字体搭配**：极简 Logo 搭配无衬线字体效果最佳
