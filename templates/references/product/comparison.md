# Before/After 对比

## 适用场景
产品效果对比、优劣对比、使用前后的变化展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A side-by-side (or top-bottom) comparison image showing the {before/after scenario} of {product/scene description}. The LEFT/TOP panel shows {before state — negative/undesired condition} with {specific visual details of the problem}. The RIGHT/BOTTOM panel shows {after state — positive/desired outcome} with {specific visual details of the improvement}. A clear dividing line or visual transition separates the two panels. Consistent composition, camera angle, and lighting between both panels ensures a fair comparison. {Background consistency}. Before and after comparison, product comparison shot, side-by-side visualization.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 护肤精华使用前后对比，左边皮肤暗沉有痘印，右边皮肤透亮光滑
**Prompt：**
```
A side-by-side comparison image showing the before-and-after results of using a premium vitamin C serum on facial skin. The LEFT panel shows the "before" state: skin with visible dark spots, uneven tone with reddish acne marks on the cheek area, dull and tired-looking complexion with enlarged pores around the nose. The lighting is flat and unflattering, slightly cool-toned, emphasizing the skin concerns. The RIGHT panel shows the "after" state: noticeably brighter, more even-toned skin with significantly faded dark spots, reduced redness, smoother texture with refined pores, and a healthy natural glow. The lighting on this side is warm and radiant, enhancing the improved skin condition. Both panels are close-up portraits of the same woman's face from identical angles, with consistent framing that focuses on the cheek and nose area. A thin white dividing line with a subtle gradient transition separates the two panels. Both backgrounds are a soft, neutral warm grey to avoid any color cast influencing the skin tones. The left panel has a small "BEFORE" label in muted grey text; the right panel has an "AFTER" label in soft gold text. Before and after comparison, product comparison shot, side-by-side visualization.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 多功能清洁剂使用前后对比，左边台面布满油污水垢，右边台面光亮如新
**Prompt：**
```
A side-by-side comparison image showing the cleaning effectiveness of a multi-purpose kitchen cleaner on a stainless steel countertop. The LEFT panel shows the "before" state: a greasy, stained kitchen counter with visible dried food splatters, hard water mineral deposits around the faucet base, streaky smudges across the stainless steel surface, and a dull, cloudy appearance. A half-squeezed sponge sits on the counter, adding to the neglected feel. The lighting is harsh overhead fluorescent, creating unflattering glare on the greasy spots. The RIGHT panel shows the "after" state: the identical countertop now gleaming with a mirror-like shine, completely free of stains, streaks, and water marks. The stainless steel surface reflects the kitchen window and overhead pendant lights beautifully. A single drop of water beads perfectly on the clean surface, demonstrating the protective residue. The lighting on this side is warm natural daylight streaming through the window, highlighting the pristine surface. Both panels are shot from the exact same angle — a slightly elevated 45-degree view looking down the length of the counter toward the faucet and backsplash. A clean white dividing line separates the two panels. The left has a "BEFORE" label; the right has an "AFTER" label, both in bold sans-serif. Before and after comparison, product comparison shot, side-by-side visualization.
```

### 示例 3

The teeth have a smooth, polished appearance with natural translucency at the edges. The lighting on this side is slightly brighter and cooler, enhancing the whitened appearance. Both panels are tight close-ups focusing on the mouth and smile area, with the lips, skin tone, and facial expression identical between panels. A subtle diagonal dividing line with a soft gradient blend transitions between the two sides. The left panel has a small "7 DAYS BEFORE" label; the right panel has "AFTER 7 DAYS OF USE" label, both in clean, modern typography. Before and after comparison, product comparison shot, side-by-side visualization.

## 注意事项
- 左右或上下分屏，构图、角度、光线必须一致
- "before" 展示问题/负面状态，"after" 展示效果/正面状态
- 对比效果要明显但不能过度夸张
- 分界线要清晰但不突兀
- 标签标注清楚（BEFORE / AFTER）
- 适合效果验证、用户见证、详情页对比模块
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9 左右分屏）
