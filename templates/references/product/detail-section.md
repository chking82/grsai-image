# 详情页模块

## 适用场景
产品详情页内容模块、规格参数展示、产品细节放大。

## Prompt 结构
```
A product detail close-up photograph of {specific part/feature of product description}, captured in extreme macro detail. The image reveals {specific texture/material/craftsmanship detail} that is normally invisible to the naked eye. {Lighting description — how light reveals the detail}. {Camera/lens description for macro effect}. {Background description — typically clean, neutral}. The shot emphasizes {quality attribute — precision, material quality, craftsmanship, etc.}. Product detail section, e-commerce detail page module, close-up product photography.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 高端面料纹理特写，展示精纺羊毛西装面料的细腻织纹
**Prompt：**
```
A product detail close-up photograph of a luxurious worsted wool suiting fabric in deep charcoal grey, captured in extreme macro detail revealing the intricate twill weave pattern that is normally invisible to the naked eye. The image shows individual wool fibers — each with a subtle natural crimp — interlaced in a precise diagonal herringbone pattern with thread counts visible at this magnification. The fabric has a soft, matte surface with a faint lustrous sheen where the light catches the fibers at the correct angle. The lighting is soft, directional side light (raking light) that creates microscopic shadows between the weave ridges, making the three-dimensional texture pop with remarkable depth and tactility. Captured with a 100mm macro lens at f/8 for optimal detail sharpness across the textured surface, with just enough depth of field to show the fabric's natural drape softening at the edges. The background is a clean, neutral warm grey that complements the fabric color without competing for attention. The shot emphasizes the exceptional quality of the textile, the precision of the weaving, and the premium hand feel of the material. Product detail section, e-commerce detail page module, close-up product photography.
```

The lighting is a combination of overhead diffused light and a subtle side reflector that creates crisp specular highlights on the metal edges and reveals the concentric machining marks on the port's inner rim. Captured with a macro lens at high magnification, with the focus plane precisely set across the connector opening and the contact pins, while the surrounding chassis surface gradually softens. The background is a deep, clean black that makes the metallic details stand out dramatically. The shot emphasizes the precision manufacturing, the durability of the materials, and the attention to detail in the product's construction. Product detail section, e-commerce detail page module, close-up product photography.
```

A product detail close-up photograph of a hand-stitched leather wallet edge, captured in extreme macro detail revealing the artisan craftsmanship of traditional saddle stitching. The image shows individual stitches of thick, waxed linen thread in rich cognac brown passing through perfectly spaced pre-punched holes along the edge of a full-grain vegetable-tanned leather panel. The lighting is warm, directional side light at a shallow angle that rakes across the stitching, creating tiny shadows in each stitch hole and emphasizing the three-dimensional relief of the thread against the leather surface. Captured with a 100mm macro lens at f/5.6, with the focus running precisely along the stitch line while the leather grain on either side softens beautifully. The background is a warm, neutral cream canvas texture that complements the leather tones. The shot emphasizes the handcrafted quality, the traditional artisan technique, and the superior durability of saddle stitching compared to machine sewing. Product detail section, e-commerce detail page module, close-up product photography.
```

## 注意事项
- 使用微距摄影风格，展示肉眼通常看不到的细节
- 侧光/斜光（raking light）最能突出表面纹理
- 对焦平面要精准，景深控制得当
- 背景干净中性，不干扰细节
- 强调材质品质、工艺精度、制造标准
- 适合详情页的"细节展示"、"品质说明"模块
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）或 2048x2048
