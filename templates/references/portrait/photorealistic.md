# photorealistic — 写实肖像

## 适用场景
品牌创始人、人物专访、真实感肖像摄影。

## Prompt 结构
```
A photorealistic portrait of [age]-year-old [gender] [ethnicity] [role], [detailed physical description], [attire], [pose and framing], [lighting description], [background], natural skin texture with pores and subtle imperfections, [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 科技创始人肖像
**Prompt：**
```
A photorealistic portrait of a 35-year-old East Asian male tech founder, short textured black hair with slight wave, dark brown eyes with subtle catchlight, light stubble, genuine warm smile looking directly at camera, wearing a charcoal gray crew-neck merino sweater, medium close-up from mid-chest, soft natural window light from right side with subtle reflector fill on left, shallow depth of field, blurred modern office interior background with warm tones, photorealistic portrait, professional photography, DSLR quality, natural skin texture, editorial portrait, visible skin pores, authentic expression, shot on Canon EOS R5 with 85mm f/1.4 lens --ar 1:1
```

## 质量后缀
```
photorealistic portrait, professional photography, DSLR quality, natural skin texture, editorial portrait, authentic skin details, high resolution, shot on full-frame camera
```

## 注意事项
- 真实皮肤质感：毛孔、细微瑕疵、自然纹理都要保留
- 避免过度磨皮或塑料感
- 专业摄影品质：光影层次丰富
- 眼神交流或适度偏移，传达情感
- 可用镜头信息增加真实感（85mm, 50mm等）
