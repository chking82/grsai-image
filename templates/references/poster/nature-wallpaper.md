# Nature Wallpaper

## 适用场景

自然风景壁纸：山川湖海、森林草地、日出日落、星空银河等自然风光类桌面壁纸和背景图。

## Prompt 结构

```
[自然景观描述] + [光线/天气条件] + [层次感/纵深] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

Majestic mountain landscape at sunrise with layers of peaks fading into atmospheric perspective. Golden sunlight breaking through morning mist illuminating the snow-capped summit. A crystal-clear alpine lake in the foreground perfectly mirroring the mountain and sky. Lush green meadows dotted with wildflowers leading the eye from foreground to distant peaks. Rich depth from foreground detail to vanishing point creating an immersive sense of scale. nature landscape photography, scenic wallpaper, high-resolution landscape, National Geographic quality, golden hour lighting, atmospheric perspective, ultra-wide angle, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Enchanted forest scene with tall ancient trees creating a natural canopy overhead. Shafts of dappled sunlight filtering through dense green leaves creating a cathedral-like atmosphere. A gentle stream meandering through moss-covered rocks with ferns and wild mushrooms along the banks. Soft fog settling between tree trunks adding mystery and depth. The composition naturally frames a clean central area suitable for desktop widget placement. nature landscape photography, scenic wallpaper, high-resolution landscape, enchanted forest photography, dappled light, ethereal atmosphere, macro detail with wide context, 16:10 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Breathtaking ocean sunset with dramatic clouds painted in violet, magenta, and burnt orange reflecting on the calm water surface. Gentle waves creating silky smooth textures in the foreground with rocks silhouetted against the glowing horizon. A lone sailboat visible in the far distance adding a sense of scale and tranquility. Long exposure effect rendering the water surface silky smooth. The vast sky dominates the upper two-thirds providing ample negative space for overlay content. nature landscape photography, scenic wallpaper, high-resolution landscape, ocean sunset photography, long exposure water, dramatic cloud formation, tranquil seascape, 21:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
nature landscape photography, scenic wallpaper, high-resolution landscape
```

可选增强：`National Geographic quality, golden hour lighting, atmospheric perspective, ultra-wide angle, ethereal atmosphere, dramatic cloud formation, tranquil seascape`

## 注意事项

- **自然光效**：黄金时刻（golden hour）、蓝色时刻（blue hour）、丁达尔效应（light rays filtering through）是提升质感的关键
- **层次感**：通过前景（岩石、花草）、中景（水面、树木）、远景（山峰、天空）构建纵深感和沉浸感
- **沉浸感**：使用 "ultra-wide angle"、"atmospheric perspective"、"immersive" 等词强调宽广的视野和代入感
- **桌面适配**：壁纸需考虑桌面图标和小部件的放置区域，在 prompt 中加入 "clean central area" 或 "negative space"
