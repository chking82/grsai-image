# 使用场景图

## 适用场景
产品功能演示、人物与产品互动、展示产品使用方式。

## Prompt 结构
```
A dynamic action shot showing {person description} actively using {product description} in a {setting/environment}. The product is being {specific action/interaction}. {Lighting and atmosphere description}. Natural hand positioning and realistic body posture showing the product in use. {Camera angle and perspective}. The image captures the moment of {action/result}, conveying {intended feeling/benefit}. Real-world product demonstration with authentic lifestyle photography style.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 女士使用大功率吹风机吹头发，在明亮的浴室里
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic action shot showing a young woman with shoulder-length dark hair actively using a professional-grade high-power hair dryer in a sleek chrome and white tile bathroom. The hair dryer — a matte black cylindrical body with a concentrator nozzle attached — is held at a 45-degree angle, directing warm airflow toward the left side of her hair, which is visibly lifted and styled by the air stream. Her fingers naturally separate strands of hair as she works. Bright natural daylight pours in from a frosted window behind her, backlighting the scene and creating a soft halo effect on the airborne hair strands. The bathroom mirror behind her shows a soft reflection of the scene. Shot from a 3/4 rear angle with a 50mm lens, medium depth of field keeping the hair dryer and her hands in sharp focus while the bathroom fixtures softly blur. The image captures the moment of effortless styling, conveying power, convenience, and salon-quality results at home. Real-world product demonstration with authentic lifestyle photography style.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 咖啡机正在萃取浓缩咖啡，咖啡液流入透明玻璃杯
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic action shot showing a sleek espresso machine with a brushed stainless steel body actively extracting a rich espresso shot. Two streams of deep amber-brown coffee with a velvety golden crema pour from the portafilter spouts into a small clear double-walled glass cup resting on the machine's warming tray. The espresso machine's pressure gauge needle points toward the optimal extraction zone, and a subtle wisp of steam rises from the group head. Warm ambient café lighting creates rich reflections on the stainless steel surfaces and the glass cup. Shot in a tight close-up with a 85mm macro lens, extremely shallow depth of field focusing on the flowing espresso streams and the emerging crema layer while the machine's body and controls artfully blur. The image captures the satisfying moment of fresh extraction, conveying precision engineering, café-quality coffee at home, and the sensory pleasure of the espresso ritual. Real-world product demonstration with authentic lifestyle photography style.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 健身者戴着无线耳机在户外跑步
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic action shot showing an athletic man in his late 20s wearing lightweight wireless earbuds with a compact charging case clipped to his running belt, sprinting along a tree-lined jogging trail during golden hour. The earbuds — sleek white stems with silicone ear tips — are clearly visible in both ears, with the right earbud catching the warm sunset light as he turns his head slightly. His running posture is natural: arms swinging, focused expression, light sweat on his brow. The trail curves through a park with autumn foliage in the background, warm orange and gold tones. Golden hour sunlight creates a rim light around his silhouette and the earbuds. Shot with a 135mm telephoto lens from the side, panning to create a slight motion blur in the background while the runner and the earbuds remain tack-sharp. The image captures the moment of energized movement, conveying freedom, motivation, and the joy of wireless audio during exercise. Real-world product demonstration with authentic lifestyle photography style.
```

## 质量后缀
```
action shot, dynamic usage scene, real-world product demonstration, lifestyle photography
```

## 注意事项
- 产品必须被使用中的状态，不是静态摆放
- 加入人物手部动作、使用姿态
- 展示产品与人的互动关系
- 光线要自然，符合场景环境
- 人物表情和肢体语言要真实
- 突出产品功能带来的效果/变化
- 动作要有动感（如飘动的头发、流动的液体等）
- 适合功能展示页面、使用说明、广告视频缩略图
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）或 2048x2048
