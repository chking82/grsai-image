# Corporate Poster

## 适用场景

商务海报：企业年报、商业计划书封面、专业展示、投资者关系材料、商务会议背景等正式商业场景。

## Prompt 结构

```
[商务场景/构图] + [几何图形/品牌元素] + [稳重点缀] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A refined corporate poster background featuring a sophisticated dark navy gradient with subtle geometric patterns of overlapping translucent hexagons in slate gray and silver. A clean diagonal band of soft white light cuts across from top-left to bottom-right, creating natural hierarchy. Ample blank space in the upper half for company name and headline, with a structured grid area at the bottom for key metrics or bullet points. Conservative yet modern design that conveys trust, stability, and innovation. corporate design quality, professional business presentation, clean layout, executive aesthetic, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Premium corporate visual with a minimalist composition of precisely arranged rectangular blocks in deep forest green, warm charcoal, and brushed gold on a pristine white background. The geometric arrangement suggests growth charts and data visualization abstractly. Sharp edges and generous white space create a sense of clarity and focus. Large open zone on the left two-thirds for presentation title, with a reserved sidebar area on the right for company logo and tagline. Boardroom-ready elegance. corporate design quality, professional business presentation, minimalist premium aesthetic, sharp vector-style composition, 4:3 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Executive-level corporate background featuring a subtle topographic map pattern in muted blue-gray tones on a warm off-white surface. A single bold accent line in deep teal runs vertically along the left edge as a branding element. The vast majority of the composition remains clean and uncluttered, providing maximum flexibility for text overlays and data charts. Understated authority and quiet confidence expressed through restraint and precision. corporate design quality, professional business presentation, understated elegance, precision design, 16:10 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
corporate design quality, professional business presentation
```

可选增强：`clean layout, executive aesthetic, minimalist premium aesthetic, sharp vector-style composition, understated elegance, precision design`

## 注意事项

- **稳重配色**：深蓝（navy）、深灰（charcoal）、深绿（forest green）为主色调，搭配金色（gold）或银色（silver）点缀
- **几何图形**：使用六边形（hexagons）、矩形块（rectangular blocks）、拓扑线（topographic lines）等传达秩序感和专业感
- **专业感**：避免过于花哨的元素，强调 "clean"、"minimalist"、"understated"、"precision"
- **文字空间**：商务海报文字内容通常较多，需预留大面积 "blank space" 或 "clean zone"
