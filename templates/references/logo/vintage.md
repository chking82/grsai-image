# Vintage Logo — 复古 Logo

## 适用场景
带有怀旧感、复古风格的 Logo 设计，适合手工皮具、精酿啤酒、复古理发店、传统食品、复古服装等品牌。

## Prompt 结构
```
A vintage logo design for {brand/industry description}, featuring {typographic style — distressed serif lettering/hand-painted sign style/letterpress texture}. The design incorporates {decorative elements — ornamental lines/vintage banners/engraved details/worn edges}. {Color scheme evoking aged patina — sepia tones/faded ink/rust and cream}. Vintage brand identity, retro typography logo, aged artisan aesthetic --ar 16:9
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 精酿啤酒复古 Logo，"铁锚啤酒厂" 搭配铁锚图案，横幅绶带设计，棕褐色调
**Prompt：**
```
A vintage logo design for a craft brewery, featuring the brewery name in a hand-painted sign lettering style with slight irregular stroke widths mimicking brush-painted wood signs from the early 1900s. The text "ANCHOR BREWING" is rendered in a distressed serif typeface in dark sepia brown (#5C3D2E) with intentional texture variations suggesting letterpress printing — slight ink bleed effects, uneven color saturation, and minor edge roughness. Above the text, a simplified iron anchor illustration is drawn in the same sepia tone with cross-hatching shading detail on the anchor body, giving an engraved copperplate illustration feel. Below the text, a flowing ribbon banner curves upward at both ends containing the text "EST. 1897" in smaller letterpress-style type. Ornamental decorative lines flank the anchor on both sides — curved scrollwork lines with small leaf-like terminals. The entire composition has a warm aged patina effect with slight yellowing suggesting old paper, and the edges of elements show subtle wear and fading as if printed decades ago. The logo sits on a warm cream background (#F0E6D3). Heritage craft brewing aesthetic with authentic early 20th century American signage character, vintage brand identity, retro typography logo, aged artisan aesthetic.
```

## 注意事项
- **做旧不等于脏**：做旧效果要有意识地添加，不是随机噪点
- **时代感明确**：维多利亚时期、1920年代、1970年代各有不同特征
- **手绘感**：不规则的笔画粗细和微小的歪斜能增加手工温度
- **字体选择**：衬线体比无衬线体更容易传达复古感
- **纹理层次**：纸张纹理、墨迹扩散、边缘磨损叠加使用效果更佳
- **色彩克制**：复古调色板通常不超过 3-4 色，以暖色调为主
- **信息密度**：复古 Logo 往往比现代 Logo 包含更多信息（年份、标语等）
- **现代平衡**：纯复古可能显得过时，建议适度简化以适配数字媒介
