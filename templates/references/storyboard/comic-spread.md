# Comic Spread — 漫画分镜

## 适用场景
动作漫画跨页、冒险故事分镜、战斗场景规划、漫画创作参考。用于需要动态冲击力和叙事张力的场景。

## Prompt 结构
```
A dynamic comic book spread of {scene description}, designed as {page layout — full-page splash/two-page spread/multi-panel page}. {Main action description with characters}. {Panel layout details — diagonal panels/overlapping frames/bleed panels}. {Art style — bold ink lines/halftone shading/cel-shaded/manga style}. Comic book spread, sequential art panel layout, dynamic action scene --ar 16:9
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 超级英雄漫画跨页，英雄从高楼跃下冲向反派，充满速度线，美式漫画风格
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic comic book two-page spread of a superhero leaping from a skyscraper rooftop diving toward a villain below, designed as an immersive full-bleed splash page spanning both pages. The hero — a muscular figure in a red and blue suit with a flowing cape — is captured mid-air in a dynamic diving pose, one arm extended forward with an energy blast glowing blue-white from the palm, the other arm trailing behind, cape billowing upward creating a dramatic wing-like silhouette. Speed lines in white radiate from the hero's body emphasizing rapid downward motion. Below, a dark villain in a long black coat stands on a street corner looking up, hands raised with purple energy crackling between the fingers, preparing a counter-attack. The city background features towering skyscrapers on both sides creating a canyon effect, with small cars and pedestrians visible far below for scale reference. The sky transitions from sunset orange at the top to deep blue near the street level. Dynamic diagonal composition — the hero's body forms a diagonal line from upper-right to lower-left, the speed lines reinforce this angle, and the villain is positioned in the lower-left corner creating visual tension. Bold black ink outlines with cel-shaded flat colors, heavy use of shadow on the villain's side contrasted with bright highlights on the hero, halftone dot patterns in the energy effects, dramatic perspective with the buildings converging toward the center bottom. Comic book spread, sequential art panel layout, dynamic action scene.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 日本漫画风格战斗分镜，剑士对决，多个小格子展示连击动作，黑白线稿风格
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic manga-style comic page of a sword duel between two warriors, designed as a multi-panel page with six irregularly sized panels creating a sense of escalating action. Panel 1 (small, top-left): Close-up of Warrior A's eye — sharp intense gaze, a bead of sweat on the temple, grip tightening on the sword hilt visible at the bottom edge. Panel 2 (small, top-right): Warrior B's smirk, sword resting casually on the shoulder, confident relaxed posture contrasting with the tension. Panel 3 (medium, middle-left): Warrior A dashes forward — body low, sword trailing behind, speed lines blur the background, the panel itself is tilted diagonally emphasizing forward momentum. Panel 4 (medium, middle-right): The clash — both swords meeting with a massive impact effect, sparks fly outward in all directions, both characters' faces shown in profile showing exertion, a large "CLANG!" sound effect fills the gap between panels. Panel 5 (large, bottom, spanning full width): The decisive moment — Warrior A has broken through, sword extended forward having just struck, Warrior B is pushed backward with a surprised expression, the impact has cracked the ground beneath them creating a spiderweb fracture pattern. Dramatic screentone shading on Warrior B's shocked face, while Warrior A is rendered in clean white with minimal shading. All panels have thick black borders, with panels 3 and 5 breaking their borders slightly — Warrior A's sword tip extends beyond panel 3's right edge, and Warrior B's body breaks through panel 5's top border creating a 3D depth effect. Bold ink line art with heavy blacks, manga-style screentone shading (dot patterns for shadows and atmosphere), Japanese onomatopoeia sound effects integrated into the artwork, dramatic perspective and exaggerated anatomy typical of shonen battle manga. Comic book spread, sequential art panel layout, dynamic action scene.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 冒险漫画分镜，探险队发现远古遗迹入口，大场景展示宏伟的古代建筑，神秘氛围
**Prompt：**
```
A dynamic comic book full-page splash illustration of an expedition team discovering the entrance to an ancient hidden temple, designed as a dramatic wide-angle establishing shot. Three explorers stand at the foreground looking up in awe — a tall archaeologist woman holding a torch aloft, a young assistant peering over her shoulder with wide eyes, and a burly guide holding a machete and lantern. They stand at the edge of an overgrown stone staircase that descends into the earth, the steps covered in moss and vines. The staircase leads to a massive carved stone doorway approximately five times their height, framed by enormous statues of ancient guardian figures on each side — towering humanoid figures with animal heads (one jackal, one hawk) holding spears, their stone faces partially eroded but still menacing. The doorway interior glows with an eerie greenish-gold light, suggesting something mysterious within. Hieroglyphic carvings cover every surface of the temple entrance, visible as detailed pattern textures. The surrounding environment is a dense jungle — giant ferns, hanging vines, tree roots cracking through ancient stonework, and fireflies creating floating light dots in the humid air. Dramatic low-angle perspective looking up at the doorway makes it feel enormous and imposing. The lighting is primarily the warm orange torch light from below and the mysterious green-gold glow from within the temple, creating a split lighting scheme that highlights the explorers from below and the temple interior from within. Detailed ink illustration style with cross-hatching for shadows, careful architectural detail on the temple carvings, atmospheric perspective with the jungle background fading into darkness. Comic book spread, sequential art panel layout, dynamic action scene.
```

```
comic book spread, sequential art panel layout, dynamic action scene
```

## 注意事项
- **动态构图**：对角线构图比水平/垂直构图更有动感和张力
- **破格设计**：角色或武器突破面板边框可以创造 3D 深度感
- **速度线**：运动方向和速度通过线条密度和方向来表达
- **面板大小**：重要时刻用大面板，过渡/细节用小面板，形成节奏
- **视角变化**：不要所有格子都用平视，仰视/俯视/特写交替使用
- **音效文字**：拟声词（BAM! SWOOSH!）是漫画的重要视觉元素
- **黑白对比**：善用黑色块和留白创造对比，不要全靠线条
- **跨页冲击**：双页跨页适合最震撼的场景，不要滥用
