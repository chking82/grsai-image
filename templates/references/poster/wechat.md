# WeChat Article Cover

## 适用场景

微信公众号推文封面：公众号文章首图、品牌推文配图、资讯内容封面等，标准为 2.35:1 横版比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[内容主题/场景] + [2.35:1 横版构图] + [品牌/标题空间] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A striking horizontal composition for a WeChat article cover featuring a modern workspace scene with a sleek laptop displaying a glowing data visualization dashboard. Soft ambient lighting from a large window casting warm afternoon tones across the desk. A small potted plant and a notebook with a pen add human touches to the tech-forward scene. The center area of the composition is kept clean and uncluttered — the softly blurred background provides perfect contrast for white or dark article title text. Professional yet approachable technology and business content aesthetic. WeChat article cover image, professional content visual, clean composition for title overlay, modern workspace photography, warm ambient lighting, technology and business theme, horizontal banner format, 2.35:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Elegant lifestyle article cover with a beautifully arranged breakfast spread on a sunlit wooden table. Fresh croissants, berries, a cup of pour-over coffee, and morning light streaming through sheer curtains creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The composition uses a shallow depth of field with the foreground sharp and the background gently blurred. The upper horizontal band remains clean and uncluttered — a smooth wall and window area in soft focus — perfect for article headline placement. The warm golden tones and lifestyle quality make readers want to click and read more. WeChat article cover image, lifestyle content visual, beautiful food photography, warm morning light, shallow depth of field, clean headline zone at top, inviting lifestyle atmosphere, 2.35:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Bold educational content cover featuring an abstract visual metaphor for growth and learning — a seedling sprouting from an open book against a vibrant gradient background transitioning from deep teal to warm orange. The composition is centered and symmetrical with the seedling as the clear focal point. Generous clean space on both sides of the central image for article title and subtitle text. The gradient background provides excellent readability for overlaid text. The visual metaphor is instantly understood and emotionally engaging. WeChat article cover image, educational content visual, visual metaphor composition, centered symmetrical layout, vibrant teal-to-orange gradient, excellent text readability, emotionally engaging design, 2.35:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
WeChat article cover image, professional content visual, clean composition for title overlay
```

可选增强：`modern workspace photography, warm ambient lighting, shallow depth of field, inviting lifestyle atmosphere, vibrant gradient, excellent text readability`

## 注意事项

- **2.35:1 比例**：微信公众号封面推荐比例为 2.35:1（约 2350×1000px），这是平台显示效果最佳的横版尺寸
- **标题文字空间**：公众号会在封面上叠加文章标题，prompt 中需明确 "clean area for title overlay"、"uncluttered zone for headline"
- **品牌一致性**：公众号通常有固定的品牌色和视觉风格，可在 prompt 中指定品牌色系以保持系列文章的统一性
- **点击吸引力**：封面图需要足够吸引眼球以促使读者点击，主体要鲜明、色彩要有吸引力、构图要有视觉焦点
