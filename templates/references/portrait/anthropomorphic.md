# anthropomorphic — 拟物化头像

## 适用场景
拟人化动物、品牌 IP 动物形象、角色化头像。

## Prompt 结构
```
An anthropomorphic character portrait of a [animal type] [wearing/doing what], [physical features of animal], [human-like features], [clothing and accessories], [pose and expression], [background], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 穿西装的猫（程序员 IP）
**Prompt：**
```
An anthropomorphic character portrait of a charming gray tabby cat standing upright like a human, feline face with distinctive tabby M marking on forehead, large intelligent amber cat eyes with slight smirk expression, wearing a well-fitted dark gray business suit with white dress shirt and a red patterned tie, a silver laptop-shaped lapel pin on the suit jacket, one paw holding a small coffee mug with steam rising, upright humanoid posture from waist up, soft warm office lighting, blurred modern tech office background with computer monitors visible, anthropomorphic character, humanized animal design, cute animal wearing clothes, character illustration, friendly mascot style, tech company brand IP quality --ar 1:1
```

## 质量后缀
```
anthropomorphic character, humanized animal design, cute animal wearing clothes, character illustration, friendly mascot style
```

## 注意事项
- 动物+人类特征结合：保留动物头部和毛发，加入人类姿势和着装
- 服装配饰要与角色职业/人设匹配
- 职业设定要清晰：通过服装、道具传达
- 亲和力是关键：表情温暖友善
- 保持动物品种的核心识别特征
