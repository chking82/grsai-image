# Beverage Label — 饮料标签

## 适用场景
精酿啤酒标签、咖啡包装标签、果汁饮料标签、茶饮标签。用于瓶/罐/袋装饮料的标签设计。

## Prompt 结构
```
A beverage label design for {product description}, applied to {container type — glass bottle/aluminum can/growler/bottle}. The label features {main visual element — illustration/pattern/typography/photo}. {Label shape — rectangular/wraparound/neck label/die-cut custom shape}. {Color scheme and typography}. {Style notes}. Beverage label design, drink bottle label, craft beer label --ar 4:5
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 精酿啤酒标签，"风暴 IPA"，海浪和闪电插画，深蓝色+橙色，复古风格
**Prompt：**
```
A beverage label design for a craft IPA beer called "STORM IPA", wrapped around a 650ml amber glass growler bottle. The main label is a large wraparound paper label covering the body of the bottle, with a separate smaller neck label above it. The main label has a dramatic illustration of a stormy ocean scene — massive dark blue waves with white foam crests crashing upward, and a bolt of lightning splitting through dark storm clouds above, rendered in a bold vintage woodcut/engraving style with heavy cross-hatching and strong contrast. The waves are primarily deep navy (#1B2A4A) with bright white foam, while the lightning is a vibrant orange (#FF6B2C) that cuts through the dark sky creating a striking focal point. The brand name "STORM IPA" is displayed in large distressed serif uppercase letters across the center of the wave illustration in white with a subtle drop shadow for readability. Below the name, smaller text reads "INDIA PALE ALE · 6.8% ABV · 65 IBU" in a clean sans-serif. The neck label features a simplified lightning bolt icon in orange on a navy background. The label paper has a subtle textured finish suggesting craft paper. The amber glass bottle is visible at the top and bottom edges of the label, creating a warm contrast with the cool-toned label. Bold aggressive craft beer aesthetic with vintage engraving influence, powerful ocean energy conveying the bold hoppy character of an IPA, beverage label design, drink bottle label, craft beer label.
```

Below the brand name, a simple white line-drawing of a coffee dripper (pour-over cone) is shown with three drops falling from the bottom — clean geometric icon design. Near the bottom of the can, in small organized grid format, key product information is displayed: "100% ARABICA", "SINGLE ORIGIN · COLOMBIA", "COLD BREWED 18 HOURS", "0 SUGAR · 0 CALORIES" — each in its own small white box with black text creating a transparent nutritional aesthetic. The top of the can near the pull-tab has a small white ring indicating "OPEN HERE" with an arrow. The overall design is extremely minimal — the black can body serves as the background and only white typography and line art are used. Modern specialty coffee aesthetic with Scandinavian minimalism influence, the design treats the can as a design object rather than just packaging, beverage label design, drink bottle label, craft beer label.

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 有机果汁标签，玻璃瓶，手绘水果插画，牛皮纸标签，天然质朴风格
**Prompt：**
```
A beverage label design for an organic cold-pressed juice on a clear glass bottle (500ml) with a swing-top closure. The main label is a die-cut irregular shape — not a perfect rectangle but an organic shape that follows the contour of the fruit illustration, printed on textured kraft brown paper (#C9A96E) that shows visible fiber texture suggesting natural recycled material. The label covers approximately 60% of the bottle's body and wraps around slightly. The dominant visual is a large hand-painted watercolor illustration of the fruit — a mango split open showing the golden-orange flesh and flat seed, rendered with characteristic watercolor bleeding where the orange and yellow tones blend softly at the edges. The mango illustration occupies about 70% of the label area. Overlapping the top portion of the mango, the product name "MANGO SUNRISE" is written in a friendly hand-lettered script font in dark brown (#3E2723), and below the mango, "ORGANIC COLD-PRESSED JUICE" in a clean small sans-serif. A small circular "USDA ORGANIC" seal stamp is placed in the upper right corner of the label in green (#2D5A27). Additional details along the bottom edge in small text: "No Added Sugar · Not From Concentrate · 500ml". The clear glass allows the actual juice color (bright golden yellow) to complement the mango illustration. Natural organic aesthetic with visible paper texture, hand-painted illustration, and warm earth tones conveying pure natural ingredients, beverage label design, drink bottle label, craft beer label.
```

## 注意事项
- **容器匹配**：标签设计要考虑容器形状——圆柱瓶要设计环绕变形，罐子有弧度
- **信息层级**：品牌名（最大）> 产品名 > 类型/口味 > 酒精度/容量 > 成分
- **法规要求**：酒精饮料必须标注酒精度、容量、健康警示；食品必须标注成分和保质期
- **湿环境**：饮料标签要防水防冷凝水——材质选择和覆膜很重要
- **货架效果**：考虑在货架上的视觉效果——颜色对比度和远距离可识别性
- **系列一致性**：同一系列不同口味用相同版式+不同颜色/图案来区分
- **材质质感**：牛皮纸标签=天然，铜版纸=现代，金属质感=高端
- **开瓶体验**：标签不要覆盖开瓶区域，瓶颈标签是增加品牌触感的额外空间
