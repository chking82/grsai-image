# animal — 动物头像

## 适用场景
宠物头像、可爱动物、拟人化动物角色。

## Prompt 结构
```
A cute animal portrait of [animal type] with [distinctive features], [fur/color description], [expression], [pose and framing], [background], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

A cute animal portrait of a golden retriever dog, rich golden cream colored fur with soft fluffy texture, warm gentle brown eyes looking directly at camera with loving expression, tongue slightly sticking out in a happy dog smile, ears flopped down adorably, close-up headshot from shoulders up, soft warm natural lighting with gentle highlights on the golden fur, blurred warm green garden background suggesting outdoor setting, cute animal portrait, pet photography style, adorable character illustration, soft fur texture, expressive eyes, heartwarming companion --ar 1:1

## 质量后缀
```
cute animal portrait, pet photography style, adorable character illustration, soft fur texture, expressive eyes
```

## 注意事项
- 动物特征准确度：品种特征要正确（耳朵形状、毛色等）
- 毛发质感是关键：柔软、蓬松、光泽感
- 可爱表情：大眼睛、微微张嘴、耳朵姿态
- 温暖配色：柔和背景色
- 胸部以上或头部特写最适合做头像
