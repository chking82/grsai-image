# Product Packaging — 产品包装设计

## 适用场景
商品包装视觉设计、电商产品包装展示、品牌包装方案提案。用于展示产品包装的外观和质感。

## Prompt 结构
```
A professional product packaging design for {product description}, featuring {package type — box/bottle/pouch/bag/tube}. The design showcases {visual elements — pattern/illustration/typography/photo}. {Color scheme and material finish — matte/glossy/metallic}. {Label details and text placement}. Product packaging design, commercial packaging visual, package design mockup --ar 4:5
```

The jar is approximately 6cm in diameter and 5cm tall with smooth rounded edges. A subtle geometric wave pattern in very faint rose gold runs across the lower third of the jar, barely visible adding depth without overwhelming the minimal aesthetic. The jar sits on a clean white surface with soft studio lighting creating gentle shadows, photographed from a slight three-quarter angle showing both the front label and the gold lid. Clean luxury cosmetic packaging aesthetic with premium materials feel, minimalist design philosophy where less is more, product packaging design, commercial packaging visual, package design mockup.

## 注意事项
- **材质质感**：磨砂、光面、金属、玻璃、牛皮纸——材质直接影响品牌感知
- **标签层次**：品牌名 > 产品名 > 卖点/规格 > 详细信息，信息要分级
- **色彩策略**：食品用暖色/鲜艳色，科技用冷色/简洁色，奢侈品用金/黑/白
- **多角度展示**：包装设计需要正面、侧面、45度角等多个视角
- **环境搭配**：产品包装要配合场景（化妆品配大理石台面，食品配木质桌面）
- **法规信息**：实际包装背面需要有成分、保质期、条形码等信息
- **尺寸比例**：包装设计要考虑货架尺寸——手机屏幕上的小缩略图是否可识别
- **系列感**：同一品牌的不同产品要有统一的包装语言（相同字体、布局、材质）
