# Weibo Social Media Post

## 适用场景

微博配图：微博九宫格图片、话题配图、品牌微博帖子、热点事件配图、产品推广微博等，标准为 1:1 正方形比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[社交话题/主体] + [1:1 正方形构图] + [视觉中心/吸引力] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

Eye-catching square composition featuring a dramatic close-up of a steaming bowl of authentic Chinese noodles. Rich broth with visible chili oil creating an appetizing glossy surface. Fresh green onions and cilantro scattered on top adding vibrant color contrast. The bowl is placed on a dark wooden table with a pair of chopsticks resting alongside. The tight crop and centered composition make the dish the undeniable star — perfect for grabbing attention in a fast-scrolling social feed. The warm food tones against the dark background create maximum visual impact. Weibo social media post, Chinese microblog post image, food photography, appetizing close-up, centered square composition, warm food styling, dark wood contrast, scroll-stopping visual, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Bold topic discussion cover featuring an abstract composition of floating Chinese characters and modern icons against a vibrant gradient background. The characters and icons are arranged in a dynamic circular pattern creating a sense of energy and conversation. The gradient flows from electric blue to warm orange creating visual excitement. The center of the circle is left relatively clean for a focal hashtag or topic title. The design feels like a trending topic visual — energetic, modern, and designed to stop the scroll. Weibo social media post, Chinese microblog post image, trending topic visual, dynamic circular composition, electric blue to orange gradient, modern graphic design, hashtag-friendly layout, conversation energy, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Product showcase square image featuring a premium wireless earbuds case resting on a clean white surface with dramatic overhead lighting creating a crisp shadow pattern. The earbuds and case are the sole focus with nothing else in the frame. The minimalist composition and strong lighting create a gallery-quality product photography feel. Ample white space around the product for adding promotional text, pricing information, or brand tags in post-production. Clean, premium, and instantly recognizable as a high-quality product. Weibo social media post, Chinese microblog post image, premium product photography, overhead flat-lay, minimalist composition, dramatic overhead lighting, ample white space, gallery-quality product shot, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
Weibo social media post, Chinese microblog post image
```

可选增强：`scroll-stopping visual, trending topic visual, conversation energy, premium product photography, minimalist composition, gallery-quality product shot`

## 注意事项

- **1:1 比例**：微博九宫格和单图发布均以 1:1 正方形展示效果最佳，信息流中不会被裁剪
- **视觉中心**：微博信息流滚动速度快，图片必须在 1 秒内传达核心信息，使用 "centered composition"、"undeniable focal point" 强化视觉中心
- **话题感**：微博是话题驱动的社交平台，配图需要与话题紧密相关，可以使用 "trending topic visual"、"conversation energy" 等描述
- **九宫格适配**：如果用于九宫格系列图，确保每张图有独立的视觉焦点，但整体风格统一
