# Instagram Social Media Post

## 适用场景

Instagram 帖子：品牌 Instagram 帖子、生活方式分享、产品展示、品牌故事视觉等，支持 4:5（竖版）或 1:1（正方形）比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[品牌/生活方式场景] + [Instagram 美学风格] + [比例适配留白] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A stunning Instagram-worthy lifestyle shot featuring a beautifully arranged desk setup on a sunny morning. A MacBook displaying a minimalist wallpaper, a ceramic mug of artisan coffee, a small stack of aesthetic notebooks, and a tiny succulent plant — all arranged with carefully considered negative space. Warm natural light from a large window creating soft shadows and a cozy yet productive atmosphere. The top third of the composition is a clean softly-lit wall area, perfect for adding brand text or motivational quotes. The aesthetic is clean, aspirational, and unmistakably Instagram. Instagram social media post, branded content visual, modern social aesthetic, lifestyle flat-lay photography, warm natural lighting, clean desk styling, aspirational workspace vibe, generous negative space for text, 4:5 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Minimalist product photography for a luxury skincare brand featuring a single bottle standing on a smooth beige stone surface. The background is a seamless warm gradient from cream to soft taupe. A single beam of directional light creates a dramatic shadow and highlights the bottle's elegant form. The composition is deliberately sparse — just the product, the surface, and the light. Small clean areas at the top and bottom for brand name and product description overlays. Understated luxury that speaks through restraint. Instagram social media post, branded content visual, modern social aesthetic, luxury product photography, minimalist composition, directional dramatic lighting, seamless gradient background, premium brand aesthetic, clean overlay zones, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Vibrant travel Instagram post capturing a stunning infinity pool edge merging seamlessly with a tropical ocean horizon. Palm trees framing the left and right edges creating a natural vignette. The water is impossibly blue and still, perfectly reflecting the cloudless sky. A lone lounge chair at the edge of the pool adds a sense of scale and invitation. The composition naturally divides into thirds — pool in the lower third, horizon in the middle, sky in the upper third — with the sky area providing perfect clean space for travel-related text overlays. Wanderlust-inducing and endlessly savable. Instagram social media post, branded content visual, modern social aesthetic, luxury travel photography, infinity pool scene, tropical horizon, natural framing with palms, rule of thirds composition, clean sky zone for text, 4:5 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
Instagram social media post, branded content visual, modern social aesthetic
```

可选增强：`lifestyle flat-lay photography, luxury product photography, minimalist composition, luxury travel photography, premium brand aesthetic, wanderlust-inducing`

## 注意事项

- **4:5 或 1:1 比例**：Instagram 信息流支持 4:5 竖版（最大展示面积）和 1:1 正方形， Stories 为 9:16。4:5 在信息流中占据更多屏幕空间，推荐优先使用
- **Instagram 风格**：Instagram 美学偏向精致但不做作（"aspirational yet authentic"），强调自然光、精心构图、高品质摄影感
- **品牌一致性**：品牌账号需要保持统一的色调和构图风格，可在 prompt 中指定品牌色系
- **可保存性**：好的 Instagram 图片让人想点击保存（"savable"），需要足够的视觉吸引力和实用价值
