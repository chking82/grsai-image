# Iconic Logo — 图形 Logo

## 适用场景
以抽象或具象图形符号作为品牌标识，不依赖文字即可传达品牌含义。适合需要高度识别度的品牌。

## Prompt 结构
```
An iconic logo design for {brand/industry description}, featuring {core symbol description — abstract geometric form/animal silhouette/combined concept}. The symbol uses {visual technique — negative space/overlapping shapes/continuous line/simplified geometry}, {color scheme}. Symbolic brand mark, abstract logo icon, instantly recognizable visual identity --ar 1:1
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 抽象图形 Logo，两个交叠的圆环代表连接与合作，蓝紫渐变色，扁平化
**Prompt：**
```
An iconic logo design for a business networking platform, featuring two perfectly circular rings that overlap to create a unified interlocking symbol. The left ring is a vibrant blue (#2563EB) and the right ring is a rich purple (#7C3AED), with the overlapping region naturally creating a blended indigo area that visually represents the concept of connection and collaboration. The rings are drawn with uniform thick strokes approximately 25% of the ring diameter, with flat solid fills and no gradients or shadows. The rings are positioned so their centers are offset horizontally by approximately 60% of the ring diameter, creating a distinctive Venn diagram-like intersection. Clean geometric construction with pixel-perfect circles on a pure white background, the intersection area clearly visible and well-proportioned. Modern corporate technology aesthetic that conveys partnership, network, and synergy through simple universal geometry, scalable from app icon to billboard, symbolic brand mark, abstract logo icon, instantly recognizable visual identity.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 动物图标 Logo，极简狐狸头部轮廓，正面视角，橙色单色，几何切割风格
**Prompt：**
```
An iconic logo design for a digital security company, featuring a highly simplified geometric fox head in direct frontal view constructed entirely from sharp triangular and diamond shapes. The fox head is built from seven distinct geometric facets — a central diamond forming the face, two triangles for the ears pointing upward at 45-degree angles, two triangles for the cheeks angling outward, and two smaller triangles forming the jaw. All facets share crisp clean edges with no overlapping, creating an origami-like flat-folded paper aesthetic. The entire mark is rendered in a warm burnt orange (#E07A2F) on a white background, with thin white gaps of uniform width separating each facet to create subtle dimensionality. The fox eyes are represented by two small white triangular cutouts within the central face diamond, giving the fox an alert intelligent expression. The overall silhouette is a tall diamond shape with pointed ears creating a distinctive recognizable profile even at small sizes. Sharp modern geometric style conveying intelligence and cunning — traits associated with both foxes and cybersecurity, symbolic brand mark, abstract logo icon, instantly recognizable visual identity.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 连续单线 Logo，一个心形中融入心跳波线，红色，适合医疗品牌
**Prompt：**
```
An iconic logo design for a health monitoring brand, featuring a continuous single unbroken line drawing that forms a heart shape whose lower half seamlessly transitions into an electrocardiogram heartbeat pulse line. The line begins at the top center dip of the heart, sweeps down and around to form the left heart curve, continues downward into a sharp EKG waveform pattern — up spike, down valley, secondary up spike — then rises back up to complete the right heart curve and return to the starting point, creating one continuous flowing stroke with no breaks or gaps. The line has consistent medium thickness throughout, drawn in a warm coral red (#E53E3E) on a clean white background. The heart proportion is slightly wider than tall for a modern look, and the EKG waveform occupies the bottom 40% of the heart shape with three distinct peaks of varying heights mimicking a real cardiac rhythm reading. The design balances emotional warmth (heart shape) with clinical precision (EKG waveform), instantly communicating health monitoring and cardiac care, symbolic brand mark, abstract logo icon, instantly recognizable visual identity.
```

## 注意事项
- **含义要清晰**：图形必须能让人一眼看懂传达的信息
- **简洁至上**：图形 Logo 越简单越容易记忆和绘制
- **避免行业陈词滥调**：科技公司不一定非要用电路/齿轮，寻找独特视角
- **单色测试**：好的图形 Logo 在纯黑或纯白下依然有效
- **尺寸适应**：必须在 16px favicon 尺寸下保持可识别
- **动物/物体选择**：选择与品牌特性有隐喻关联的符号
- **负空间巧用**：FedEx 的隐藏箭头就是负空间的经典案例
- **避免过度细节**：毛发、纹理等细节在小尺寸下会糊成一团
