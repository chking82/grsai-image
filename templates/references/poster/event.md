# Event Poster

## 适用场景

活动海报：科技峰会、产品发布会、行业大会、论坛展览等正式商业活动的宣传海报设计。

## Prompt 结构

```
[主体场景/氛围] + [排版留白区域] + [风格描述] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A sleek, modern event poster background for a global technology summit. A grand futuristic stage with holographic data streams and glowing geometric shapes floating in the air. Deep navy blue and electric cyan color palette. Clean open space in the upper center for event title text placement, secondary blank area at the bottom for date and venue information. Minimalist yet impactful composition with subtle light rays. professional event poster design, commercial event marketing, high-resolution, photorealistic, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Dynamic conference poster design featuring a striking abstract composition of interconnected light nodes forming a network pattern. Rich gradient from dark indigo to vibrant purple and gold accents. Dramatic spotlight effect illuminating the center area. Generous negative space at the top third for headline text, with a clean rectangular zone at the bottom for sponsor logos. Premium corporate event aesthetic with sophisticated visual hierarchy. professional event poster design, commercial event marketing, editorial quality, studio lighting, 4:5 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Bold product launch poster with an explosive burst of crystalline particles radiating from the center. Deep black background with neon blue and white highlights creating a sense of revelation and innovation. Subtle grid pattern overlay for a tech-forward feel. Large unoccupied area in the upper half for product name and tagline, lower section reserved for event details and QR code space. Clean, premium, attention-grabbing. professional event poster design, commercial event marketing, commercial photography quality, ultra-detailed, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
professional event poster design, commercial event marketing
```

可选增强：`editorial quality, studio lighting, ultra-detailed, photorealistic, 8k resolution`

## 注意事项

- **文字区域留白**：必须在 prompt 中明确要求 "open space", "blank area", "negative space" 用于文字排版
- **主题表达**：根据活动类型调整色调——科技峰会用蓝/青色，产品发布用黑/金色，行业会议用深蓝/紫色
- **视觉冲击力**：使用光效、粒子、几何等元素创造层次感和视觉焦点
- **比例规范**：线下海报常用 4:5 或 3:4，线上宣传常用 16:9
