# 3D角色

## 适用场景
3D 游戏角色设计、品牌 3D 代言人、动画电影角色概念、产品 3D 虚拟形象。适合需要立体感和品质感的角色创作。

## Prompt 结构
```
A 3D {character type} of {character description}, rendered in a {animation style — Pixar/Disney/game} style. The character has {body proportions} with {skin/material texture} and {hair/fur description}. Wearing {clothing/outfit description}. The character is {pose/action} with a {facial expression} expression, conveying {emotion}. {Lighting setup — e.g., soft key light, rim light, ambient fill}. {Environment/background description}. High-quality 3D rendering with {technical quality details — subsurface scattering, ray-traced reflections, volumetric lighting}. 3D character render, Pixar-style animation, soft subsurface scattering, octane render.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 冒险游戏主角，年轻的精灵弓箭手，银白色长发，绿色斗篷，森林背景
**Prompt：**
```
A 3D elf archer character, a young female adventurer with elongated elegant ears, silver-white hair flowing past her shoulders in gentle waves, and luminous emerald-green eyes. The character has stylized proportional features — slightly oversized eyes and a refined facial structure — rendered in a Pixar-style animation aesthetic. She is wearing a forest-green hooded cloak with leather trim and a brown leather quiver strapped across her chest, the fletching of three arrows visible over her shoulder. The character stands in a confident ready stance, one hand resting on the grip of a wooden longbow, with a determined yet warm expression. Soft key lighting from upper-left creates gentle highlights on her hair strands and the fabric folds of the cloak, with a warm rim light separating her silhouette from the background. Set in an enchanted forest clearing with dappled sunlight filtering through the canopy, mossy tree trunks, and floating firefly particles in the air. High-quality 3D rendering with soft subsurface scattering on the skin, ray-traced reflections on the bowstring, and volumetric god rays through the foliage. 3D character render, Pixar-style animation, soft subsurface scattering, octane render.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 品牌 3D 代言人，圆润可爱的机器人，白色机身配蓝色发光眼睛，挥手打招呼的姿势
**Prompt：**
```
A 3D robot mascot character, a round and lovable tech ambassador with a smooth white metallic body, oversized glowing sapphire-blue circular eyes, and a friendly curved LED mouth display showing a warm smile. The character has stubby articulated arms and legs with visible but simplified joint mechanisms, rendered in a Pixar-style animation aesthetic with soft, approachable proportions. One arm is raised in an enthusiastic waving gesture, fingers splayed in a cheerful greeting. The white body panels have a soft matte finish with subtle blue accent lines tracing the seams, and the chest features a subtle glowing power indicator. Soft studio-quality three-point lighting with a large key light creating gentle highlights on the curved surfaces, a cool blue fill light adding depth, and a warm rim light creating a subtle halo effect. Set against a clean gradient background transitioning from soft blue at the top to white at the bottom, with a subtle reflective floor surface showing a faint reflection of the character. High-quality 3D rendering with soft subsurface scattering on the white panels, ray-traced reflections on the metallic joints, and carefully crafted specular highlights that emphasize the smooth, premium material feel. 3D character render, Pixar-style animation, soft subsurface scattering, octane render.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 奇幻 RPG 矮人铁匠，壮硕身材，红色大胡子，皮革围裙，手里拿着发光的铁锤

```
A 3D dwarf blacksmith character from a fantasy RPG, a burly and muscular figure with a thick braided red beard and bushy eyebrows, rendered in a stylized Pixar-like animation aesthetic. The character has exaggerated broad shoulders and a stocky build, wearing a soot-stained leather apron over a simple linen shirt, with metal bracers on both forearms. He holds a massive forge hammer in his right hand, the hammer head glowing with orange embers and heat distortion effects. The character's expression is proud and boisterous — a wide grin with a missing front tooth, one eyebrow raised in a mischievous look. Dramatic warm lighting from below simulating forge fire glow, casting flickering orange highlights on the character's face and beard, with a cooler blue fill from above representing ambient workshop light. Set inside a dimly lit blacksmith workshop with a stone wall backdrop, glowing forge coals visible in the background, and floating sparks and ash particles in the air. High-quality 3D rendering with soft subsurface scattering on the skin, volumetric lighting through the forge smoke, and carefully textured materials — weathered leather, rough linen, and polished metal. 3D character render, Pixar-style animation, soft subsurface scattering, octane render.
```

## 注意事项
- 强调 3D 渲染质感：提到 subsurface scattering（次表面散射）让皮肤真实
- 灯光描述要具体：三点布光、边缘光、体积光等
- 材质描述很重要：金属、布料、皮肤的质感差异
- 比例可以夸张（大眼睛、小鼻子）但要保持 3D 可信度
- 适合游戏、动画、品牌 IP、虚拟代言人
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048 或 2048x1152
