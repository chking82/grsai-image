# Four-Panel Comic — 四格漫画

## 适用场景
社交媒体分享、品牌故事讲述、日常幽默内容、教育/科普讲解。四格漫画结构紧凑、易于传播。

## Prompt 结构
```
A four-panel comic strip arranged in a 2x2 grid layout, reading {left-to-right/top-to-bottom}. {Panel 1 description}. {Panel 2 description}. {Panel 3 description}. {Panel 4 description with punchline/resolution}. {Art style — clean line art/cute chibi/semi-realistic/hand-drawn sketch}, {color style — full color/black and watercolor accent/muted palette}. Four-panel comic strip, sequential art, daily comic illustration --ar 1:1
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 职场幽默四格漫画，程序员日常——"代码写完、测试通过、上线成功、第二天客户说功能不对"
**Prompt：**
```
A four-panel comic strip arranged in a 2x2 grid layout, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Panel 1 (top-left): A young programmer sits at a desk with two monitors, typing furiously with a confident smile. Code fills the screens. A coffee cup and energy drink sit nearby. The programmer thinks "Almost done! This code is perfect!" Panel 2 (top-right): The programmer hits the Enter key dramatically with a big grin. The screen shows "BUILD SUCCESSFUL — ALL TESTS PASSED" in bright green text. Confetti-like sparkles float around the keyboard area. Panel 3 (bottom-left): The programmer is relaxing in a chair with feet on the desk, sipping coffee peacefully. A calendar on the wall shows "LAUNCH DAY" with a big checkmark. The programmer thinks "Nothing can go wrong now." Panel 4 (bottom-right): The programmer is slumped over the keyboard with wide terrified eyes as a phone on the desk shows an incoming message: "Client: Hey, the button doesn't do what we discussed." A giant sweat drop appears on the programmer's head. The same coffee cup from panel 1 now has a crack in it. Clean digital art style with cute semi-chibi character proportions, soft pastel color palette with warm tones, clear panel borders with slight drop shadow, speech bubbles with clean rounded corners. Four-panel comic strip, sequential art, daily comic illustration.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 日常搞笑四格，猫咪的一天——睡觉、要吃的、继续睡、凌晨三点跑酷
**Prompt：**
```
A four-panel comic strip arranged in a 2x2 grid layout, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Panel 1 (top-left): A fat orange tabby cat is curled up in a perfect donut shape on a sunlit windowsill, peacefully sleeping with small "Zzz" symbols floating above. Sunlight streams through the window creating a warm golden glow. The clock on the wall reads 10:00 AM. Panel 2 (top-right): The same cat is now sitting upright next to its empty food bowl, looking up at the camera with enormous pleading puppy-dog eyes. The food bowl has exactly one piece of kibble left. The cat thinks "I haven't eaten in HOURS" (despite the full food bag visible in the background). Panel 3 (bottom-left): The cat is back on the windowsill in the exact same donut sleeping position as panel 1, clock now reads 2:00 PM. Panel 4 (bottom-right): Chaos scene at night — the clock reads 3:00 AM in dark blue tones. The same orange cat is zooming across the room at full speed, knocking over a vase (water splashing), jumping off the couch (cushions flying), and running past a sleeping human whose eyes are wide open in exasperation. Motion lines everywhere. Clean hand-drawn digital illustration style with expressive cartoon cat features, warm daytime colors for panels 1-3 contrasting with cool dark blue tones for panel 4, clean panel borders. Four-panel comic strip, sequential art, daily comic illustration.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 科普教育四格，解释为什么天空是蓝色的，用两个小朋友对话
**Prompt：**
```
A four-panel comic strip arranged in a 2x2 grid layout, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom, designed as an educational science explanation. Panel 1 (top-left): Two children — a curious girl with glasses (age 8) and her older brother (age 12) — sit on a grassy hill looking up at a bright blue sky with fluffy white clouds. The girl points upward and asks "Why is the sky blue?" in a speech bubble. Panel 2 (top-right): The brother holds up a prism and a flashlight, demonstrating. A beam of white light enters the prism and emerges as a rainbow spectrum. A small simplified diagram shows sunlight with different colored wave lines — short blue waves and long red waves. The brother explains "Sunlight has all colors mixed together!" Panel 3 (bottom-left): A simplified cross-section of the atmosphere is shown — the earth below, the atmosphere layer above with tiny dots representing air molecules. Blue wave lines (short wavelength) are shown bouncing off the air molecules in all directions, while red wave lines (long wavelength) pass straight through. The brother explains "Air scatters blue light everywhere!" Panel 4 (bottom-right): Back to the children on the hill. The girl looks up at the sky with an enlightened expression, light bulb above her head. She says "So the blue is bouncing around up there!" Both children are smiling. Clean educational illustration style with cute character designs, bright cheerful colors, clear diagrams with labeled elements, speech bubbles and small caption boxes for explanations, child-friendly and accessible art. Four-panel comic strip, sequential art, daily comic illustration.
```

## 注意事项
- **2x2 网格**：四格漫画经典布局，阅读顺序从左到右、从上到下
- **起承转合**：第一格起（设定）、第二格承（发展）、第三格转（转折）、第四格合（结局/笑点）
- **角色一致**：同一角色在四个格子中要保持外观一致
- **对话框清晰**：对话框不要遮挡关键画面内容
- **格子边框**：清晰的面板分隔线帮助读者区分场景
- **教育类**：科普四格中，示意图要简洁易懂，避免过多文字
- **色彩对比**：可以用不同色调区分不同情绪（如温馨 vs 混乱）
