# 饮料标签

## 适用场景
饮料瓶标签、酒类标签、咖啡包装标签。

## Prompt 结构
```
A close-up product photography shot of {beverage container description} featuring a {label style/design description} label. The label design includes {label elements — typography, illustrations, brand logo, color scheme}. {Container material and shape details — glass curve, condensation, liquid color}. {Lighting description — backlighting for liquid transparency, side lighting for label texture}. {Background description}. The photograph captures the interplay between {design aesthetic} and {container character}. Beverage label design, drink packaging photography, bottle label visualization, product label close-up.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 精酿啤酒瓶标签，复古风格插画，深棕色玻璃瓶，金色酒液透光
**Prompt：**
```
A close-up product photography shot of a 330ml craft beer bottle made of deep amber-brown glass, featuring an elaborately illustrated vintage-style label wrapping around the bottle's body. The label design is inspired by 19th-century engraving art — a detailed copperplate-style illustration of hops vines, barley stalks, and a shield-shaped brand emblem with the brewery name "Iron Creek Brewing Co." printed in a classic serif typeface with ornamental flourishes. The color palette of the label uses deep forest green, warm copper, and cream-white on a textured kraft paper stock with visible deckled edges. The bottle's deep brown glass catches the backlight, revealing the rich golden-amber color of the beer inside, with tiny CO2 bubbles clinging to the inner glass wall. A delicate layer of condensation beads on the bottle's surface, suggesting it's been freshly chilled. The lighting is a combination of warm backlighting that makes the beer glow from within and soft side fill light that reveals the label's embossed texture and the bottle's elegant curve. Shot at eye level with a 85mm lens, shallow depth of field keeping the label text and illustration razor-sharp while the bottle neck and base softly blur. The background is a dark, warm charcoal with subtle wood grain texture, evoking a craft brewery tasting room. The photograph captures the interplay between heritage-inspired artisan design and the refreshing character of a premium craft brew. Beverage label design, drink packaging photography, bottle label visualization, product label close-up.
```

Between the text lines, a small, elegant line-art illustration of a coffee cherry branch adds a touch of craft. The bag's matte white surface has a subtle tactile texture, and the top edge is neatly folded and sealed with a small branded sticker. The bag stands upright, photographed from a slight 3/4 angle to show both the front label and the gusseted side. The lighting is soft, diffused natural daylight from the left, creating gentle shadows that give the bag volume and dimension without harsh contrast. Shot with a 50mm lens at medium distance, deep enough depth of field to keep the entire label and bag clearly readable. A close-up product photography shot of a 700ml single malt Scotch whisky bottle crafted from deep emerald green glass with a heavy, premium-feeling base and a long, elegant neck. The main label is a rich cream-colored paper with intricate gold foil stamping — the distillery name "Glenmorangie" rendered in an ornate Victorian-era serif typeface with decorative borders, a detailed engraving-style illustration of the Scottish Highlands with mountains and a loch, and the age statement "Aged 18 Years" prominently displayed. A subtle layer of dust-free glass clarity shows the bottle's pristine condition. The lighting is dramatic and luxurious — a warm key light from above creates a golden highlight on the bottle shoulder, a rim light from behind outlines the bottle silhouette and reveals the green glass color, and a soft fill from below eliminates harsh shadows on the label. Shot with a 100mm macro lens at a slight upward angle, shallow depth of field focused on the label's gold text and illustration. The background is a deep, dark wood grain surface transitioning to near-black, evoking a gentleman's study or whisky lounge. The photograph captures the interplay between centuries-old Scottish whisky tradition and the luxurious presentation of a premium aged spirit. Beverage label design, drink packaging photography, bottle label visualization, product label close-up.

## 注意事项
- 标签设计细节要清晰可见（字体、图案、品牌 Logo）
- 瓶身弧度和材质要精准表现（玻璃、铝罐、纸袋等）
- 液体透光效果是饮料标签的关键视觉元素
- 背光（backlight）能突出饮料颜色和透明度
- 冷凝水珠可以增加真实感和新鲜感
- 不同饮料类型有不同的风格标签（精酿=复古、精品咖啡=极简、威士忌=经典奢华）
- 适合产品详情页、电商主图、品牌宣传
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048（方图）或 2048x1152
