# team-series — 团队头像系列

## 适用场景
公司团队展示、一致风格的多人头像。

## Prompt 结构
```
A professional team portrait headshot of [age]-year-old [gender] [ethnicity] [role], [physical features], [attire in team style], chest-up framing, [consistent lighting], [consistent background], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 创业团队 5 人头像（成员 1：CEO）
**Prompt：**
```
A professional team portrait headshot of a confident 35-year-old East Asian male startup CEO, short neat black hair, warm engaging dark eyes, genuine leadership smile, wearing the team uniform of a navy blue blazer over a white t-shirt with no tie, chest-up framing with slight shoulder angle toward camera, consistent soft studio lighting with large softbox key light from 45 degrees and subtle fill light, consistent light gray gradient background matching team series, team portrait series, consistent style across portraits, corporate team photography, matching background and lighting, professional quality --ar 1:1
```

## 质量后缀
```
team portrait series, consistent style across portraits, corporate team photography, matching background and lighting, professional quality
```

## 注意事项
- 风格统一：相同的布光方案、背景、着装规范
- 背景一致：纯色或统一渐变背景
- 每个人特征鲜明：通过发型、表情、小配饰区分
- 统一姿势：胸部以上、微微侧身
- 适合排列展示：统一画幅（1:1）和裁剪
- 可在统一框架下允许个人化元素（首饰、眼镜等）
