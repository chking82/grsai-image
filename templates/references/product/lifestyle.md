# 生活方式场景

## 适用场景
品牌故事、社媒推广、产品使用场景展示。

## Prompt 结构
```
A lifestyle product photography scene of {product description} placed in a {scene/environment}. {Scene details including props, natural elements, and atmospheric elements}. {Lighting description — natural light, warm tones, etc.}. The composition feels authentic and lived-in, not staged. {Camera perspective and depth of field}. Editorial quality, warm and inviting atmosphere that tells a story about {brand mood / lifestyle theme}.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 保温杯放在木质餐桌旁，旁边有咖啡杯和一本翻开的书，早晨阳光
**Prompt：**
```
A lifestyle product photography scene of a stainless steel insulated thermos bottle with a brushed silver finish, placed on a rustic oak dining table alongside a ceramic coffee mug and an open hardcover book. Morning sunlight streams through a nearby window, casting soft golden light rays across the table surface and creating gentle highlights on the thermos lid. A small potted succulent sits in the background, slightly out of focus. A woven linen placemat and a half-eaten croissant on a small plate add to the lived-in, authentic feel of the scene. Natural diffused window light with warm color temperature, soft shadows, and a cozy morning atmosphere. Shot from a slightly elevated angle with a 50mm lens, medium depth of field keeping the thermos sharp while the book pages and coffee mug softly blur. Editorial quality, warm and inviting atmosphere that tells a story about a mindful, unhurried morning routine.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 护肤品套装摆放在浴室大理石台面上，有绿植和蜡烛
**Prompt：**
```
A lifestyle product photography scene of a curated skincare set — a frosted glass serum bottle, a matte white cream jar, and a soft cotton facial towel — arranged on a polished white marble bathroom vanity counter. A small trailing pothos plant cascades from a ceramic shelf above, adding fresh green tones. A single lit pillar candle in a frosted glass holder creates a warm amber glow near the products. The background features large-format white subway tiles and a frameless mirror reflecting soft ambient light. Natural daylight supplemented by the candle's warm glow creates a spa-like, serene atmosphere. Shot at eye level with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field keeping the serum bottle and cream jar in sharp focus while the mirror and tiles gently soften. Editorial quality, warm and inviting atmosphere that tells a story about self-care rituals and mindful skincare routines.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 无线蓝牙音箱放在公园野餐毯上，旁边有野餐篮和水果
**Prompt：**
```
A lifestyle product photography scene of a compact cylindrical wireless Bluetooth speaker in sage green fabric finish, resting on a red-and-white gingham picnic blanket spread across a sunlit meadow. A woven wicker picnic basket sits open nearby with fresh strawberries, a wedge of brie cheese, and a baguette peeking out. Wild daisies and grass blades frame the edges of the composition. Dappled sunlight filters through the canopy of a large oak tree overhead, creating natural patterns of light and shadow across the blanket and speaker. Shot from a low angle with a 35mm lens, moderate depth of field capturing the speaker clearly while the surrounding picnic items create a soft, dreamy context. Editorial quality, warm and inviting atmosphere that tells a story about outdoor adventures, friendship, and enjoying music in nature.
```

## 质量后缀
```
lifestyle product photography, editorial quality, authentic atmosphere, commercial lifestyle imagery
```

## 注意事项
- 场景要自然、生活化，避免过度摆拍的感觉
- 加入环境元素（植物、光线、织物、食物等）增强氛围
- 描述具体的光线条件（窗光、阳光、烛光等）
- 产品是场景的一部分，而非绝对中心
- 加入人物元素时保持自然姿态（手部、背影等）
- 构图要有故事感和情感连接
- 色调通常偏暖、偏自然
- 适合社交媒体、品牌故事、内容营销
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x2048（方图适合 Instagram）
