# 卖点展示图

## 适用场景
详情页卖点模块、功能特点展示、参数可视化。

## Prompt 结构
```
A commercial product imagery showcasing {specific feature/benefit} of {product description}. The visual composition highlights {feature detail} with {visual technique — icon, callout, graphic element, etc.}. {Product position and angle}. {Background and lighting}. Clean information hierarchy with {text/icon/graphic placement} that makes the benefit immediately understandable. Feature showcase, product benefit visualization, commercial product imagery.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 防水智能手表，展示IP68防水等级，手表在水中
**Prompt：**
```
A commercial product imagery showcasing the IP68 waterproof capability of a premium smartwatch with a round sapphire crystal face and titanium case. The visual composition highlights the watch fully submerged in crystal-clear water, with tiny air bubbles rising from the edges and light rays refracting through the water creating shimmering caustic patterns on the watch face. The watch screen is still illuminated, displaying the time and heart rate reading, proving full functionality underwater. Positioned at a slight upward angle so the viewer sees both the watch face and the water surface above. Set against a deep aqua-blue gradient background that transitions from light cyan at the surface to deep ocean blue below. Clean information hierarchy with a subtle translucent badge in the lower-left corner showing "IP68 Waterproof · 50M Depth" in crisp white text, accompanied by a minimalist water droplet icon. The water surface is visible at the top of the frame with natural ripple patterns. Bright, clear lighting from above simulates underwater sunlight. Feature showcase, product benefit visualization, commercial product imagery.
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** 充电宝，展示20000mAh大容量，可为手机充电5次
**Prompt：**
```
A commercial product imagery showcasing the massive 20,000mAh battery capacity of a slim portable power bank with a matte aluminum unibody finish in space gray. The visual composition places the power bank in the center, with a smartphone positioned above it connected by a sleek USB-C cable, and a visual energy flow indicator — a glowing gradient line transitioning from the power bank through the cable to the phone — suggesting rapid charging in progress. Around the power bank, five faint translucent phone silhouettes are arranged in an arc, each with a small battery icon showing 100%, visually representing the "charges your phone 5 times" benefit. Positioned flat on a dark graphite surface. Set against a deep charcoal background with a subtle radial glow emanating from the power bank, emphasizing it as the energy source. Clean information hierarchy with a bold "20,000mAh" callout positioned to the right of the product in a modern sans-serif font, with "5x Full Charges" beneath it in a smaller secondary style. A minimalist battery icon with five filled segments sits next to the text. Dramatic side lighting creates a metallic sheen on the aluminum body and casts a sharp, clean shadow. Feature showcase, product benefit visualization, commercial product imagery.
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 降噪耳机，展示主动降噪功能，外部噪音被隔绝
**Prompt：**
```
A commercial product imagery showcasing the active noise cancellation capability of premium over-ear wireless headphones with a deep matte black finish and memory foam ear cushions. The visual composition splits the frame conceptually — the left side shows chaotic, jagged red and orange sound wave lines representing loud urban noise (traffic, construction, crowd chatter), which hit an invisible barrier at the headphone's outer shell and dissipate. The right side shows the interior of the earcup area with smooth, gentle blue wave patterns representing pure, undisturbed audio. The headphones are shown in a profile view, floating in the center of the composition, with the noise-canceling boundary visualized as a subtle translucent shield shimmering around each earcup. Set against a split background — turbulent warm tones on the noise side transitioning to calm cool tones on the quiet side. Clean information hierarchy with a bold "-35dB ANC" badge positioned near the headphones in a modern floating card design, accompanied by a minimalist crossed-out noise icon. Dramatic directional lighting emphasizes the headphone's premium contours and creates a striking contrast between the chaotic and calm zones. Feature showcase, product benefit visualization, commercial product imagery.
```

## 质量后缀
```
feature showcase, product benefit visualization, commercial product imagery
```

## 注意事项
- 每个视觉对应一个明确的卖点
- 信息层级要清晰，主卖点突出，次要信息辅助
- 可以加入图标、文字标注、数据可视化元素
- 用视觉隐喻传达功能（如水=防水、波浪=降噪）
- 产品仍然是主体，功能元素围绕产品
- 适合详情页的功能特点模块
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）
