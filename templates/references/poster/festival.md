# Festival Poster

## 适用场景

节日海报：春节、圣诞节、中秋节、国庆促销、双十一购物节等节日庆典和促销活动的视觉设计。

## Prompt 结构

```
[节日主题/氛围] + [装饰元素/图案] + [喜庆配色] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A vibrant Chinese New Year celebration poster with a rich crimson and gold color scheme. Ornate paper-cut style decorative border featuring traditional Chinese motifs — dragons, lanterns, and plum blossoms. Subtle golden particle effects drifting across a warm gradient background from deep red to bright coral. A central clean circular area reserved for main promotional message or event title. Festive decorations scattered throughout including red envelopes (hongbao) and firecracker silhouettes. Joyous, prosperous, and culturally rich atmosphere. festive event poster, celebration themed design, holiday marketing visual, traditional Chinese New Year aesthetic, rich cultural elements, 4:5 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Enchanting Christmas holiday poster with a deep midnight blue background dusted with soft snowflakes. Warm golden fairy lights strung across the top in gentle sweeping curves. A frosted pine branch frame in the corners with subtle bokeh light effects creating a magical winter wonderland feel. Large open rectangular space in the center for holiday greeting or promotional offer. Delicate silver accents and a touch of crimson ribbon for classic Christmas color harmony. festive event poster, celebration themed design, holiday marketing visual, magical Christmas atmosphere, warm winter lighting, 1:1 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Energetic shopping festival poster with an explosive composition of colorful confetti and ribbon streamers in hot pink, electric blue, golden yellow, and vivid orange. Dynamic diagonal layout suggesting motion and excitement. A bold gradient backdrop transitioning from magenta to orange to yellow. Clean banner area across the upper portion for sale headline, with product showcase space in the lower half. High-energy, irresistible shopping mood with irresistible visual pull. festive event poster, celebration themed design, holiday marketing visual, explosive energy, shopping festival aesthetic, bold promotional design, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
festive event poster, celebration themed design, holiday marketing visual
```

可选增强：`rich cultural elements, magical Christmas atmosphere, explosive energy, shopping festival aesthetic, bold promotional design`

## 注意事项

- **节日元素**：根据具体节日融入标志性元素——春节（灯笼、剪纸、红包、生肖）、圣诞（雪花、彩灯、松枝）、中秋（圆月、玉兔、桂花）
- **喜庆氛围**：使用高饱和度暖色调（红、金、橙、粉），配合粒子效果、彩纸、丝带等动态元素
- **装饰图案**：可采用传统纹样（回纹、祥云、缠枝纹）或现代几何装饰增强节日感
- **促销适配**：购物节海报需要更强的视觉冲击力和 "irresistible" 的紧迫感设计
