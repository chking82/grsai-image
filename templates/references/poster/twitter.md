# Twitter/X Post Image

## 适用场景

Twitter/X 图片：推文配图、品牌 Twitter 帖子、观点分享配图、资讯推文视觉等，标准为 16:9 横版比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[推文主题/场景] + [16:9 横版构图] + [信息密度控制] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A clean and modern data visualization-style graphic showing a rising growth curve emerging from a cityscape silhouette at dawn. The background is a subtle gradient from deep navy to warm sunrise orange. The curve is rendered as a glowing line of light suggesting momentum and progress. The composition is horizontal and balanced, with the cityscape along the bottom third and the dramatic sky above providing generous clean space for overlay text or statistics. The visual communicates growth and forward momentum instantly — perfect for business or technology tweets. Twitter/X post image, social media tweet visual, modern platform aesthetic, data visualization style, rising growth curve, dawn cityscape silhouette, business and technology theme, generous text space in sky area, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

An impactful quote-style background featuring bold abstract typography shapes integrated into a textured dark gray background. The composition uses layered geometric blocks in muted gold and deep burgundy creating a sophisticated visual frame. The central area is kept clean and relatively uniform for quote text or key message overlay. The dark textured background provides excellent contrast for white text. The overall design feels editorial and thought-provoking — the kind of tweet image that makes people stop scrolling and read. Twitter/X post image, social media tweet visual, modern platform aesthetic, editorial quote background, geometric block composition, dark textured surface, excellent text contrast, thought-provoking design, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

A dynamic product announcement image showing a sleek tech gadget floating in mid-air against a clean white-to-light-gray gradient background. Subtle shadow beneath the product grounding it in space. A soft halo of colored light (brand accent color) behind the product creating subtle depth. The horizontal composition places the product slightly off-center following the rule of thirds, with clean negative space on the opposite side for announcement text, release date, or call-to-action. Clean, modern, and announcement-ready. Twitter/X post image, social media tweet visual, modern platform aesthetic, floating product photography, clean gradient background, soft halo lighting, rule of thirds composition, clean negative space for text, product announcement style, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
Twitter/X post image, social media tweet visual, modern platform aesthetic
```

可选增强：`data visualization style, editorial quote background, floating product photography, clean gradient background, product announcement style`

## 注意事项

- **16:9 比例**：Twitter/X 推文中的图片最佳展示比例为 16:9（1200×675px），横版构图适配信息流展示
- **信息密度适中**：Twitter 用户同时阅读文字推文和图片，图片不宜过于复杂或信息过载。强调 "clean"、"balanced"、"generous space" 确保图片与推文文字互补而非竞争
- **即时传达**：推文滚动速度极快，图片需要在瞬间传达核心信息——使用 "instantly communicates"、"scroll-stopping" 等描述
- **可叠加文字**：Twitter 配图常叠加统计数据、引用语、产品发布信息，需预留 "clean space for text overlay"
