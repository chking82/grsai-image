# Q版系列

## 适用场景
表情包贴纸系列、办公场景趣味插画、心情状态系列、社交媒体内容、团队文化建设。适合需要可爱、亲和力的角色系列设计。

## Prompt 结构
```
A set of {number} chibi character illustrations featuring {character description}, designed as a {series theme} sticker/expression set. Each chibi has {proportions — large head, small body, simplified features} with {key visual elements — hair, clothing, accessories}. The characters display {range of emotions/poses}, each with exaggerated {expression details}. Style: {art style description}. {Background treatment — consistent background per sticker, transparent, or themed scenes}. Clean line art, vibrant colors, sticker-ready formatting. Chibi character series, cute proportional design, kawaii expression set, sticker-ready illustration.
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 办公场景 Q 版系列，上班族角色，6 种不同表情：开心工作、疲惫加班、摸鱼发呆、会议犯困、收到奖金、被老板叫去
**Prompt：**
```
A set of 6 chibi character illustrations featuring an office worker character, designed as a workplace emotion sticker series. Each chibi has exaggerated kawaii proportions — an oversized round head taking up half the body, tiny simplified limbs, dot eyes with large highlight circles, and a small curved mouth. The character wears a light blue dress shirt, dark grey tie, and black slippers. Each illustration captures a distinct workplace emotion: (1) "Happy Working" — character typing enthusiastically at a tiny desk with sparkles and musical notes floating around, big smile, star-shaped eyes. (2) "Exhausted Overtime" — character slumped over the keyboard, drooling slightly, dark circles under eyes, the clock on the wall shows midnight, muted grey-blue color tint. (3) "Slacking Off" — character leaning back in the chair with feet on the desk, phone in hand, a speech bubble with a game icon, mischievous half-smile. (4) "Sleepy in Meeting" — character sitting at a conference table, head nodding forward, "Zzz" bubbles, coffee cup knocked over, eyes barely open. (5) "Got a Bonus" — character jumping in the air with arms raised, tears of joy, giant smile, money symbols and confetti everywhere. (6) "Called by Boss" — character standing nervously, sweating, wide frightened eyes, a shadowy figure with a speech bubble saying "Come to my office." Clean line art with thick outlines, vibrant flat colors, each sticker on a transparent background with a white border outline. Chibi character series, cute proportional design, kawaii expression set, sticker-ready illustration.
```

### 示例 2

```
A set of 8 chibi character illustrations featuring an adorable round ball-shaped kitten, designed as a daily mood expression sticker series. Each chibi has ultra-simplified kawaii proportions — an almost perfectly circular head-body with tiny nub-like paws, a short stubby tail, and an oversized expressive face. The cat is white with soft grey patches on the ears and one eye patch. Each illustration shows a distinct mood: (1) "Happy" — huge sparkling eyes with star highlights, wide open-mouth smile with tongue out, small hearts floating around, paws raised joyfully. (2) "Angry" — furrowed eyebrows, sharp fangs showing, puffed-up fur making the body slightly larger, a visible anger vein on the forehead, steam coming from ears. (3) "Wronged" — downturned mouth forming a wavy line, big watery eyes with tear drops, ears drooping, hugging itself sadly. (4) "Proud" — smug half-closed eyes, nose pointing up with a tiny snort, one paw on hip, a little crown floating above the head. (5) "Shocked" — eyes blown wide with tiny pupils, jaw dropped open, fur standing on end making the silhouette spiky, exclamation marks floating. (6) "Acting Cute" — tilted head, one paw raised to the cheek, big pleading eyes with shimmering reflections, small pink blush circles on cheeks, a tiny heart bubble. (7) "Sleepy" — half-closed droopy eyes, tiny yawn, a small "zzz" bubble, lying flat like a pancake with paws tucked under. (8) "Jealous" — narrowed side-eye glare, crossed arms, a green jealousy cloud, looking at another cat silhouette in the distance. Clean bold line art with thick outlines, soft pastel color palette, each sticker on a transparent background with a white stroke border. Chibi character series, cute proportional design, kawaii expression set, sticker-ready illustration.
```

## 注意事项
- Q版的核心是头身比夸张——通常 1:1 到 1:2（头占身体一半以上）
- 表情要极度夸张——眼睛、嘴巴的变形是情绪传达的核心
- 系列要保持角色一致性：同一个角色在不同表情下的识别度
- 透明背景 + 白边描线是贴纸的标准做法
- 适合做成微信表情、钉钉表情、飞书表情
- 系列数量建议 6-16 个，覆盖常用情绪场景
- 输出分辨率建议 1024x1024（贴纸常用尺寸）
