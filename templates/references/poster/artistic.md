# Artistic Poster

## 适用场景

文艺海报：文化展览、音乐节、艺术活动、戏剧演出、画展等具有强烈艺术感和创意表达的视觉设计。

## Prompt 结构

```
[艺术风格/手法] + [文化意象/元素] + [排版空间] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

An evocative artistic poster featuring hand-painted watercolor washes in deep burgundy, burnt orange, and warm gold. Abstract fluid shapes suggest musical notes and dance movements floating across a textured off-white paper background. Visible brush strokes and ink bleed effects adding authentic handmade quality. Generous open space in the upper third for exhibition title typography, with a clean vertical margin on the right for event details. Gallery-quality artistic expression with raw creative energy. artistic poster design, creative visual communication, gallery quality, hand-painted texture, watercolor on paper aesthetic, 2:3 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Surreal collage-style artistic poster combining vintage botanical illustrations with contemporary geometric overlays in muted sage green and dusty rose. Delicate line drawings of flowers and butterflies interwoven with sharp angular shapes creating visual tension. Subtle grain texture throughout for a zine-like indie feel. Large negative space at the center for artwork title, with small blank areas at corners for date and venue. Eclectic, cultured, and deeply creative. artistic poster design, creative visual communication, gallery quality, mixed media aesthetic, vintage-meets-modern, 3:4 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Expressive abstract art poster with bold gestural brushstrokes in cobalt blue, crimson, and white on a warm cream canvas. The composition suggests a dramatic landscape or seascape through pure abstraction. Thick impasto texture visible in the paint application. Clean rectangular area in the lower portion for exhibition information and artist name. Museum-worthy piece that commands attention and invites contemplation. artistic poster design, creative visual communication, gallery quality, museum-grade print, expressive abstraction, 1:1.4 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
artistic poster design, creative visual communication, gallery quality
```

可选增强：`hand-painted texture, museum-grade print, expressive abstraction, mixed media aesthetic, vintage-meets-modern`

## 注意事项

- **文艺感**：强调手绘质感（watercolor, brush strokes, ink bleed）、混合媒介（collage, mixed media）、抽象表现（gestural, abstract）
- **手绘元素**：在 prompt 中加入 "hand-painted", "watercolor", "brush strokes", "ink wash" 等关键词增强手工感
- **排版空间**：文艺海报的留白更加自由，可以是 "negative space"、"clean margin"、"open zone"，不必过于规整
- **色彩选择**：文艺风格偏好低饱和度、暖色调或单色调配色方案
