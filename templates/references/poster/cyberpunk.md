# Cyberpunk Wallpaper

## 适用场景

赛博朋克壁纸：霓虹灯光下的未来都市、雨夜中的科技城市、科幻风格的视觉设计，适用于科技产品背景、游戏相关设计、年轻潮流品牌等。

## Prompt 结构

```
[赛博朋克场景] + [霓虹色彩对比] + [雨夜/科技元素] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

A sprawling cyberpunk cityscape at night drenched in rain, with towering megastructures covered in massive holographic advertisements. Neon signs in electric pink, cyan, and acid green illuminate the wet streets below creating vivid reflections in every puddle. Flying vehicles leave light trails through the smoggy air between buildings. Dense fog and volumetric neon light beams cutting through the atmosphere. The lower third of the composition is a rain-soaked street level scene, while the upper two-thirds shows the towering architecture and holographic displays — leaving dark sky areas for text placement. cyberpunk aesthetic, neon-lit futuristic city, sci-fi atmosphere, rain reflections, holographic advertisements, flying vehicle light trails, volumetric neon lighting, dense urban atmosphere, Blade Runner inspired, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Intimate cyberpunk alley scene at night with a lone figure walking under the glow of mismatched neon signs. The narrow passage is flanked by buildings covered in layered signage in Japanese kanji, Korean hangul, and English — all glowing in different neon colors. Steam rising from a grate near the foreground catching the pink and blue light. Rain-slicked surfaces reflecting the chaotic neon palette creating a kaleidoscope of color on the ground. High contrast between the dark shadows and brilliant neon creating dramatic visual tension. cyberpunk aesthetic, neon-lit futuristic city, sci-fi atmosphere, rain reflections, narrow neon alley, layered signage, steam effects, high contrast neon lighting, dystopian urban beauty, 21:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Abstract cyberpunk background featuring a close-up view of a rain-streaked window with a blurred neon cityscape beyond. Droplets of water on the glass catching and refracting the neon lights creating beautiful chromatic aberration effects. The out-of-focus city lights form gorgeous bokeh circles in magenta, cyan, and electric blue. The rain streaks create natural vertical lines adding texture to the composition. The soft focus nature of the image makes it perfect as a background that won't compete with foreground content. cyberpunk aesthetic, neon-lit futuristic city, sci-fi atmosphere, rain reflections, rain-streaked window, chromatic aberration, neon bokeh, soft focus background, atmospheric depth of field, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
cyberpunk aesthetic, neon-lit futuristic city, sci-fi atmosphere, rain reflections
```

可选增强：`holographic advertisements, volumetric neon lighting, Blade Runner inspired, dystopian urban beauty, chromatic aberration, neon bokeh, atmospheric depth of field`

## 注意事项

- **霓虹色彩对比**：赛博朋克的标志是强烈色彩对比——粉红（pink/magenta）、青色（cyan）、酸绿（acid green）、电蓝（electric blue），与深暗背景形成强烈反差
- **雨夜氛围**：雨（rain）、反射（reflections）、雾气（fog/mist）、蒸汽（steam）是营造赛博朋克氛围的核心元素
- **科技元素**：全息广告（holographic advertisements）、飞行车（flying vehicles）、霓虹招牌（neon signs）、多层招牌（layered signage）构建未来都市感
- **宽画幅沉浸感**：赛博朋克城市景观适合超宽画幅（21:9），展现城市的压迫感和壮观
