# sketch — 素描手绘

## 适用场景
手绘肖像、铅笔素描、文艺感。

## Prompt 结构
```
A pencil sketch portrait of [subject description], [physical features], [attire or none], [pose], white or textured paper background, [drawing technique details], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 文艺创作者肖像
**Prompt：**
```
A detailed pencil sketch portrait of a 28-year-old East Asian male creative writer, medium-length slightly messy black hair, expressive dark eyes with thoughtful gaze looking slightly upward, light stubble, wearing round wire-frame glasses and a simple crew-neck sweater, three-quarter view from shoulders up, fine detailed graphite shading with careful crosshading technique for depth, visible pencil strokes and graphite texture, subtle smudging for soft shadows around eyes and cheeks, white paper background, hand-drawn illustration quality, high contrast between dark and light areas, pencil sketch portrait, detailed graphite drawing, crosshading technique, white paper background, hand-drawn illustration, artistic sketch style --ar 3:4
```

## 质量后缀
```
pencil sketch portrait, detailed graphite drawing, crosshading technique, white paper background, hand-drawn illustration, artistic sketch style
```

## 注意事项
- 线条感是关键：明确的铅笔线条和轮廓线
- 明暗对比要强烈，利用交叉排线（crosshading）
- 保留手绘痕迹：铅笔纹理、橡皮擦痕、石墨颗粒感
- 白色或微暖白色纸张背景
- 面部细节精致，周围可放松处理
