# Cityscape Wallpaper

## 适用场景

城市夜景壁纸：霓虹灯下的城市天际线、赛博朋克风格都、雨后街道倒影、繁华都市夜景等充满现代都市氛围的设计场景。

## Prompt 结构

```
[城市景观描述] + [霓虹灯效/光源] + [天气/氛围] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

Spectacular city skyline at night viewed from across a calm harbor. Towering skyscrapers illuminated in warm gold and cool blue lights creating a glittering crown against the deep navy sky. Building reflections shimmering on the dark water surface doubling the visual impact. A few scattered stars visible above the city lights. The composition places the skyline along the horizontal middle, with dark sky above providing clean space for text, and dark water below for additional content. cityscape photography, urban night scene, city skyline at night, harbor reflection, golden and blue lighting contrast, wide angle cityscape, peaceful urban nightscape, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

Rain-soaked city street at night with neon signs from storefronts and restaurants reflecting brilliantly in puddles on the asphalt. Vibrant pink, cyan, and yellow neon characters glowing through the misty rain. The perspective looks down the street with buildings on both sides creating leading lines toward a distant vanishing point. Soft bokeh from out-of-focus neon lights in the background. The upper half of the composition is relatively dark sky area suitable for overlay text, while the lower half is filled with colorful reflections and street-level energy. cityscape photography, urban night scene, city skyline at night, neon reflections in rain puddles, bokeh night photography, atmospheric rain, cyberpunk-adjacent aesthetic, moody urban atmosphere, 21:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Aerial view of a sprawling metropolis at dusk transitioning into night. The last warm orange glow of sunset on the horizon meets the emerging electric lights of millions of buildings. Major highways visible as flowing ribbons of white and red light from car headlights and taillights. The city grid creates an organic pattern of light and shadow stretching to the horizon. Dramatic cloud formation above catching the last light of day. Epic urban landscape that captures the pulse of modern civilization. cityscape photography, urban night scene, city skyline at night, aerial cityscape, highway light trails, golden hour to night transition, metropolitan panorama, dramatic twilight sky, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
cityscape photography, urban night scene, city skyline at night
```

可选增强：`harbor reflection, neon reflections in rain puddles, bokeh night photography, atmospheric rain, aerial cityscape, highway light trails, dramatic twilight sky`

## 注意事项

- **霓虹灯效**：使用 "neon signs"、"neon reflections"、"neon characters glowing" 等描述霓虹光源的独特色彩和质感
- **天际线轮廓**：强调 "skyline"、"towering skyscrapers"、"city grid" 等城市标志性轮廓元素
- **氛围光**：雨后倒影（rain reflections）、雾气（misty rain）、散景（bokeh）、光轨（light trails）是营造氛围的关键
- **宽画幅优势**：城市天际线适合超宽画幅（21:9），展现城市的广阔感和壮观感
