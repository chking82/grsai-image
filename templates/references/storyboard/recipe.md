# Recipe Comic — 料理/教程漫画

## 适用场景
美食博主内容、菜谱步骤可视化、烹饪教程漫画、食品品牌内容。用漫画形式让烹饪过程更有趣易懂。

## Prompt 结构
```
A recipe illustration comic showing {dish name} preparation in {number} sequential panels arranged in {layout — horizontal row/grid/magazine spread}. {Panel-by-panel descriptions of cooking steps}. {Art style — warm hand-drawn illustration/food manga/cute cooking comic}. {Color style — warm food photography tones/bright cheerful colors}. Recipe illustration comic, cooking step-by-step visual guide, hand-drawn cooking tutorial --ar 16:9
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 红烧肉步骤漫画，5 格，从五花肉切块到成品，手绘可爱风格，暖色调
**Prompt：**
```
A recipe illustration comic showing braised pork belly (红烧肉) preparation in 5 sequential panels arranged in a horizontal row, reading left to right. Panel 1: A pair of cute cartoon hands holds a kitchen knife cutting a thick slab of raw pork belly (with visible layers of fat and meat) into uniform 3cm cubes on a wooden cutting board. Small meat cubes are neatly arranged, steam or sparkle effects suggest freshness. The cutting board has measurement marks showing "3cm". Panel 2: The pork cubes are being blanched in a pot of boiling water on a stovetop. Bubbles rise vigorously, the pork cubes have turned slightly pale. A cartoon chef character (a small round figure with a chef hat) points at the pot with a ladle, saying "Boil 3 minutes to remove impurities!" in a speech bubble. Steam rises in wavy lines. Panel 3: A wok on high heat with rock sugar melting into a caramel-colored syrup — the sugar has turned amber and is bubbling. The pork cubes are about to be added. The caramel color is shown as a rich golden-brown liquid with glossy highlights. Panel 4: The pork cubes simmer in a rich dark braising liquid in the wok. Soy sauce bottles and a star anise pod and cinnamon stick float in the liquid. The liquid is a deep reddish-brown, small bubbles indicate gentle simmering. A timer in the corner shows "45 minutes" with an hourglass icon. Panel 5: The final plated dish — glossy dark red-brown braised pork cubes arranged beautifully on a white plate, glistening with sauce, garnished with sliced green onions. Steam rises in appetizing swirls. Chopsticks reach in from the top right to pick up one piece. A small "DELICIOUS!" exclamation with sparkles and hearts. Warm hand-drawn illustration style with cute simplified characters, the food is rendered with appetizing detail and glossy highlights, warm golden and reddish color palette throughout emphasizing the rich caramel and braised tones, soft rounded shapes and friendly character design making the recipe feel approachable and fun. Recipe illustration comic, cooking step-by-step visual guide, hand-drawn cooking tutorial.
```

## 注意事项
- **步骤清晰**：每个面板一个步骤，不要在一格中展示多个操作
- **食材标注**：关键食材和用量最好在画面中标注，方便读者参考
- **火候提示**：温度、时间是烹饪关键，用温度计、计时器等视觉元素表达
- **食物光泽**：好吃的食物一定有光泽感——高光让食物看起来更美味
- **角色引导**：加入一个引导角色（小厨师/小吉祥物）让教程更有亲和力
- **色彩温暖**：暖色调（橙、红、棕）让食物看起来更美味
- **成品特写**：最后一格一定是诱人的成品展示，这是读者的动力来源
- **安全提醒**：涉及高温操作（油炸、烤箱）可以加入安全提示图标
