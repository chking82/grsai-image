# 促销/活动图

## 适用场景
大促主图、限时活动、折扣促销、节日促销。

## Prompt 结构
```
A promotional product image featuring {product description} as the centerpiece, surrounded by {promotional elements — discount badges, countdown indicators, sale banners, festive decorations, etc.}. The product is {position and angle}, showcased with {lighting style}. {Background description — festive colors, dynamic patterns, or thematic elements}. Strong visual tension with {color palette and contrast}. {Additional promotional text/graphics placement}. Promotional product image, sale event graphic, discount campaign visual, commercial advertising.
```

The product sits on a dark reflective pedestal with its own subtle spotlight, making it the undeniable focal point amid the promotional chaos. Strong visual tension with the rich red-and-gold color palette against the black headphones. Promotional product image, sale event graphic, discount campaign visual, commercial advertising.
```

```

## 注意事项
- 促销元素要醒目但不能遮挡产品（折扣标签、倒计时、横幅等）
- 加入节日装饰或活动相关视觉元素
- 色彩通常鲜艳、对比强烈（红金、红黑等经典促销配色）
- 创造视觉张力和紧迫感
- 产品仍然是核心，促销元素围绕产品排列
- 适合大促主图、活动页面、限时促销广告
- 输出分辨率建议 2048x1152（16:9）或 2048x2048
