# founder — 创始人形象

## 适用场景
科技公司创始人、CEO 形象照、创业公司宣传照。

## Prompt 结构
```
A portrait of [age]-year-old [gender] [ethnicity] [company type] founder, [hair style], [facial features], [expression], wearing [tech-appropriate attire], [pose and framing], [lighting], [background style], [quality suffix], [aspect ratio]
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** SaaS 公司创始人
**Prompt：**
```
A portrait of a 32-year-old East Asian male SaaS company founder, medium-length styled black hair with subtle side part, dark brown eyes, confident relaxed smile looking at camera, wearing a well-fitted navy unstructured blazer over a clean white t-shirt, no tie, medium close-up from mid-chest with hands casually in pockets, soft natural light mixed with subtle studio fill, warm diffused key light from left, modern co-working space background slightly out of focus with glass walls and plants visible, tech founder portrait, modern corporate photography, approachable yet professional, startup aesthetic, natural skin texture, contemporary entrepreneurial style --ar 1:1
```

### 示例 2
**用户输入：** AI 创业团队 CEO
**Prompt：**
```
A portrait of a 38-year-old East Asian female AI startup CEO, sleek shoulder-length black bob haircut, sharp intelligent dark eyes, warm confident expression with a slight knowing smile, wearing a tailored olive green oversized blazer over a black fitted turtleneck, medium close-up framing, soft directional light from large window with warm golden hour glow, clean modern interior with abstract tech artwork on wall in soft focus background, tech founder portrait, modern corporate photography, approachable yet professional, startup aesthetic, confident natural presence, contemporary design sensibility --ar 4:5
```

### 示例 3
**用户输入：** 26岁男性独立开发者
**Prompt：**

```
A portrait of a 26-year-old Caucasian male indie developer and founder, tousled sandy brown hair, bright blue eyes, enthusiastic genuine smile, light beard and mustache, wearing a dark gray hoodie under an open olive green chore jacket, medium close-up, warm natural window light with subtle rim light, cozy home office background with desk setup and plants in shallow focus, tech founder portrait, modern corporate photography, approachable yet professional, startup aesthetic, casual creative energy, authentic indie developer vibe --ar 1:1
```

## 质量后缀
```
tech founder portrait, modern corporate photography, approachable yet professional, startup aesthetic, natural skin texture, contemporary entrepreneurial style
```

## 注意事项
- 科技行业风格：通常不系领带，休闲西装或高品质休闲装
- 自信但不刻板，展现亲和力和创造力
- 现代感：场景可以是联合办公空间、简约办公室或居家办公
- 避免传统商务照的僵硬感
- 光线温暖自然，不用过度戏剧化的布光
