# Picture Book Illustration — 绘本插图

## 适用场景
儿童睡前故事绘本、教育绘本、亲子阅读读物、儿童品牌 IP 视觉。需要温暖亲切的叙事风格。

## Prompt 结构
```
A children's picture book illustration showing {scene description}. {Main character(s) description and action}. {Setting/background details}. {Mood/atmosphere}. {Art style — gentle watercolor painting/soft colored pencil/warm gouache/ink wash}, {color palette — pastel tones/warm earth colors/soft rainbow palette}. Children's picture book illustration, warm storytelling art style, gentle illustration --ar 4:3
```

## 示例

### 示例 1
**用户输入：** 睡前故事绘本插图，小兔子在月光下寻找回家的路，沿途有发光的蘑菇和萤火虫，温馨水彩风格
**Prompt：**
```
A children's picture book illustration showing a small lost bunny wandering through an enchanted forest at twilight, looking for the way home. The main character is a tiny brown bunny with oversized floppy ears and large round dark eyes filled with a mix of wonder and slight worry, standing on hind legs with one paw raised as if pointing. The bunny wears a small red scarf that trails slightly in the breeze. The forest setting features oversized trees with thick mossy trunks and a canopy of soft indigo leaves filtering moonlight. Scattered across the forest floor are clusters of bioluminescent mushrooms in soft blue and pale green, each casting a gentle glow that illuminates the moss and ferns around them. Three fireflies float at different heights in the scene, their warm golden light creating small halos. A narrow winding path of white pebbles leads from the foreground into the background where a tiny warm yellow light suggests the bunny's home in the distance. The moon is a large gentle crescent visible through a gap in the canopy, casting silver-blue light across the scene. Gentle watercolor painting style with soft bleeding edges, translucent washes of indigo and blue for the night atmosphere, warm golden and green accents for the magical elements, soft pastel color palette with muted tones, rounded gentle shapes throughout with no sharp edges, warm storytelling art style that makes the nighttime feel safe and magical rather than scary. Children's picture book illustration, warm storytelling art style, gentle watercolor illustration.
```

## 注意事项
- **温暖安全第一**：即使是紧张场景，也要让孩子感到安全而非恐惧
- **角色夸张化**：大眼睛、大头身比、圆润轮廓——这些都是孩子觉得可爱的要素
- **色彩柔和**：避免刺眼的纯色和强烈对比，以柔和的中间色调为主
- **留白呼吸**：不要填满画面，留白让孩子有想象空间
- **细节适中**：每页有一个主要细节亮点，不要过多分散注意力
- **情感表达**：角色表情要夸张清晰，孩子通过表情理解情绪
- **媒介选择**：水彩、彩色铅笔、水粉是最传统的绘本媒介
- **跨页考虑**：如果是双页跨页，注意中间装订线区域不要放关键内容
