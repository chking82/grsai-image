# Bilibili Video Cover

## 适用场景

B站视频封面：Bilibili 视频封面、ACG 相关内容、游戏视频、知识分享、动漫解说等平台内容配图，标准为 16:9 横版比例。

## Prompt 结构

```
[视频内容/主体] + [16:9 横版构图] + [ACG 风格/视觉元素] + [质量后缀] + [比例参数]
```

## 示例 1

An exciting anime-style illustration featuring a dynamic battle scene between two stylized characters in a glowing digital arena. One character wields a brilliant sword of light while the other summons dark energy from their hands. The background is a futuristic digital landscape with floating data panels and neon grid lines. The composition places the characters in the center with clean dark areas on the left and right sides suitable for video title text overlays. Vibrant color contrast between light and dark energy effects creates maximum visual impact. Bold, energetic, and designed to attract anime fans scrolling through the feed. Bilibili video cover, Chinese video platform thumbnail, anime-friendly aesthetic, dynamic anime illustration, battle scene composition, digital arena background, light vs dark energy contrast, clean side zones for text, bold and energetic anime style, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 2

A clean and engaging knowledge-sharing video cover featuring a stylized 3D illustration of a brain with glowing neural connections on one side and a computer chip with circuit patterns on the other. The two halves are connected by flowing light streams suggesting the fusion of human intelligence and artificial intelligence. The background is a deep space gradient from dark blue to purple with subtle star-like dots. The upper portion of the composition provides clean dark space for video title text. The overall design is educational yet visually exciting — making complex topics look cool and accessible. Bilibili video cover, Chinese video platform thumbnail, anime-friendly aesthetic, 3D educational illustration, brain and AI fusion concept, deep space gradient background, glowing neural connections, clean upper text zone, visually exciting educational content, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 示例 3

Floating islands drift in the sky above, connected by glowing bridges. A massive dragon flies in the distant background. The color palette is rich and saturated — deep purples, electric blues, and warm golds creating a sense of adventure and wonder. Bilibili video cover, Chinese video platform thumbnail, anime-friendly aesthetic, epic fantasy landscape, floating islands and dragon scene, saturated color palette, adventure and wonder mood, dark lower third for text overlay, gaming community appeal, 16:9 aspect ratio --v 2

## 质量后缀

```
Bilibili video cover, Chinese video platform thumbnail, anime-friendly aesthetic
```

可选增强：`dynamic anime illustration, 3D educational illustration, epic fantasy landscape, bold and energetic anime style, visually exciting educational content, gaming community appeal`

## 注意事项

- **16:9 比例**：B站视频封面标准为 16:9（1146×717px 推荐尺寸，最小 1146×717），横版构图
- **ACG 风格**：B站核心受众是 ACG 文化爱好者，图片可以使用动漫风格（anime-style）、二次元元素、游戏美学等更年轻化的视觉语言
- **吸引点击**：B站封面的核心目标是提高点击率（CTR），需要鲜明的主体、夸张的色彩对比、充满好奇心的场景 — "designed to attract clicks"
- **标题文字区**：B站会在封面底部叠加视频时长，prompt 中需确保底部不被关键内容占据，文字区域主要在画面中上部或两侧
